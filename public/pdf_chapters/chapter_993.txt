Simultaneous Incidents (3)

~*~

Adolf XIII and the royal family emerged from the underground bunker.

The royal residence had partially collapsed, and the royals were furious—but there was no time to complain.

“What happened?” Adolf XIII demanded. “A battle took place in the capital without the king’s knowledge? Summon everyone involved immediately.”

“Your Highness, the priority right now is handling the current crisis—”

“Uncle.” Adolf’s eyes flashed with anger. “I am the king. No matter the war, what good is the state if royal authority is not restored?”

It wasn’t that he didn’t understand the anxiety of a newly crowned ruler—but that was precisely why the situation was so difficult.

*Everyone refused.*

The fact that high-ranking officials ignored his summons proved they didn’t trust him.

Robbins, Adolf XIII’s younger brother and first in line to the throne, spoke up.

“Your Highness, the people already see you as a great king. The public follows you, the politicians follow, and even the lower officials across institutions are beginning to fall in line.”

“Hmm.”

That was at least something to be glad about.

“It’s also fortunate that the Temple Army is stationed in Baska. If we position Tormia as the center of the world war, we’ll receive massive support from other nations.”

The royals nodded in agreement.

“You’re right. Especially the Southern Continent—we should demand heavy contributions. Aren’t we acting as their shield?”

Adolf XIII gave a slow nod.

“I agree. Come to think of it… I heard someone from the Ivory Tower has arrived.”

“This is Oh Dae-seong and Arian Shirone from the Unified Space Management Department. They’re from our own country and are responsible for the fraternity of the times and the great axis of the world war.”

“Heh. What’s this about philanthropy?” Adolf XIII scoffed. He didn’t believe in things he couldn’t see.

“Regardless, treat them with the utmost respect. Offer any amount of money if needed.”

After all, if the Ivory Tower recognized them, other countries would fund them through subsidies.

“Understood. But before that, there’s one urgent matter.”

“Solution?”

Robbins narrowed his eyes.

“I am Pony, 127th in the direct royal line.”

“Kuhm.” Adolf XIII’s face darkened.

“Oh Dae-seong and Pony studied together at magic school. If she captures Shirone first…”

The royals gasped, horror dawning on their faces.

“She must be eliminated! Haven’t you seen it? She cast magic without hesitation. She only stepped down from the throne to survive. Now that the city is stable, she’ll definitely make her move.”

Adolf XIII rested his chin on his hand.

“What can I do? Recruiting a 1-star resident of the Ivory Tower is hard enough—let alone Oh Dae-seong.”

“I don’t care—even the beauty world will back her.” It was a statement that undermined royal authority, but the target was Pony. No one hesitated.

“Didn’t you suspect it before? She entered the world beauty contest just to climb the royal lineage. She knows exactly how she looks. She’ll do *anything* to win.”

Now, the possibility of a coup became real.

“… Can we really do it? The Magic Society won’t help us, and military command has already passed to the Crusade. If we fail to kill her, *we’ll* be the ones killed.”

Robbins spoke calmly.

“I have a solution. Use criminals.”

“A prison?”

“The concentration camp for Class A and higher criminals is managed by the Magic Association.”

“There’s someone suitable in the western prison, Inferno.”

*I never thought I’d use this card—but I prepared it for the day I might have to kill my father or older brother.*

The royals looked at him, curious.

“Inferno? Even for the worst criminals, wouldn’t they be Class B at most?”

“They are. But… he’s not imprisoned for committing a crime.”

“I can vouch for his skill. He’s the son of Gunzo Bedium, the world’s greatest assassin. He also attended the same magic school as Shirone and Pony.”

“Gunzo Bedium… the Black Line?” The name alone made the royals’ teeth clench.

*His son is in Inferno? And how does Robbins know about him?*

For a moment, their faces paled—but they quickly composed themselves.

*Of course. Anyone in the 10th position of the royal line has their own ace.*

“Let’s not question it. For now, we must eliminate Pony first.”

No risk was greater than the threat of her enslaving Shirone.

Adolf XIII’s expression remained unchanged.

He was the king, after all.

“Good. Robbins, I’ll leave Pony to you. But this must be handled with absolute certainty.”

“Yes, Your Highness. Don’t worry.”

Just as Robbins bowed, a noise came from outside the door.

“The Great Star of the Ivory Tower requests an audience.”

The voice was urgent. The royals exchanged glances.

*Too fast?*

From Tormia’s perspective, it was a perfect opportunity—but danger often hid behind good fortune.

“Let him in.”

At the order, Robbins slipped out through the back door. The front doors opened.

Adolf XIII rose personally to greet Shirone.

“Welcome. The newly crowned king greets the stars of the Ivory Tower.”

His sincerity betrayed his desperation.

“I am Shirone, one of the Five Great Stars of the Ivory Tower.”

“My apologies for the disarray. As you can see, we’ve just endured a great upheaval—no time for panic.”

*It’s a commotion.*

Shirone didn’t react. He sat down without comment.

Ladies-in-waiting brought out high-quality tea, hastily airlifted in.

“A banquet should be held, but there’s no time to prepare…”

“That’s unnecessary.”

Shirone had no interest in ceremony.

“I came to hear the new king’s vision. Humanity stands on the edge.”

“Of course.” Adolf XIII straightened at once.

“Today, the Kingdom of Tormia achieved an unprecedented victory in the World War. We annihilated the demons and drove out the giants. This was made possible by the unity of Tormia’s people and the Temple.”

Shirone gave a single nod.

“We will announce this moment to the world. With Tormia at the center, mankind will rise again. Star of the Ivory Tower—I entrust that role to you.”

*Adolf XIII. Real name: Adolf Thenar. Age: 44.*

Shirone knew his history up to age 22, thanks to Omega.

*Ambitious since youth. His problem-solving style: swift, stealthy, brutal.*

Adolf XIII continued.

“Tormia will do everything—give its heart and soul—to support the Five Great Castles.”

It was blatant, but Shirone remembered Guffin’s words.

*Listen to everyone’s story.*

“I’ve heard your vision,” Shirone said. “Now, where is Pony? She’s not here.”

The royals’ expressions stiffened. Shirone didn’t miss it.

“Can I call her? I have something to tell her in person.”

“Of course. Pony is a truly brave member of the royal family. She’s likely out, handling matters personally. Oh Dae-seong will return soon—ugh!”

The moment he looked at Shirone, a jolt ran down his spine.

The sensation of being *seen*.

*What is this?*

He was used to keeping a perfect poker face in politics and society—but this was different. It felt like his soul was being dissected.

Shirone leaned forward.

“What are you hiding?”

Adolf XIII smiled—perfect, practiced.

“Hiding? How could I lie to the stars of the Ivory Tower?”

Shirone smiled back.

“I haven’t corrected that habit of yours yet.”

“What habit?”

“When you lie, you use that signature smile.”

“That’s absurd. I smile all the time.”

“But in certain moments, you close your eyes—just once—before opening them again. A childhood memory. Weren’t you beaten severely by your predecessor for lying?”

Silence fell over the hall.

Adolf XIII’s throat moved as he swallowed.

*How does he know that?*

It was a secret known only to him and his father.

*No—would my father even remember?*

It wasn’t something significant enough for even *him* to recall—yet Shirone knew.

“I know you trained hard to perfect that smile. But there’s one thing you never practiced…”

Shirone pointed at him.

“How many people fed the prince of Tormia honey chestnuts?”

Adolf XIII’s fist trembled on his lap.

*Is Oh Dae-seong a ghost?*

This wasn’t insight. It wasn’t analysis.

*It’s too small. Even a spirit-type mage would need a month to dig this deep.*

It was as if his soul had been stripped bare.

“I’ll give you one last chance. What are you hiding?”

Adolf XIII gambled.

“Haha. I don’t know what you mean.”

He smiled, sweat glistening on his brow—and closed his eyes again.

Baska West Prison, Inferno.

Pony arrived after receiving an urgent telegram. She handed the document, sealed with royal authority, to the warden.

“This way.”

No nation existed beyond the king’s reach. Every monarch held the power to issue pardons.

*I’ve taken on an unpleasant task.*

She had been preparing to leave Baska when Pony received an urgent call.

The order: release prisoners from Inferno. They needed manpower for national restoration.

Aware of the manpower shortage, the warden complied—but with deep skepticism about the royal family’s judgment.

“This way.”

She was led to the darkest underground level of Building E, the deepest part of Inferno.

This was where special criminals were held—each one vicious, dangerous.

*What kind of talent are they looking for here?*

She promised herself she’d return to Al Peas Magic School once this was over. Walking down the long corridor, she heard a woman’s moan from the farthest cell.

It was impossible to sleep together, yet a man’s breath mingled with the soft voice.

*What kind of person is he?*

It was common knowledge that this prisoner received special treatment due to his talent.

But Pony’s voice, already cold with disgust, cut through the air.

“Prisoner 3824. Assigned to special probation. Come out.”

The moaning stopped.

*Special probation?*

One of the five keywords Prince Robbins had given her. Its meaning: *Kill the one who comes to you.*

*It’s time.*

A voice echoed from the ceiling.

“For 30 seconds, the magic suppression device in Building E will be deactivated. Anyone acting suspiciously during the siren will be executed on the king’s orders.”

A siren blared. The suppression field vanished.

“Brother, who’s outside?”

Pony couldn’t stand the tender voice from inside the cell.

“Come out, you bastard.”

She turned the key given by the warden and opened the door.

“Ouch!”

A hand shot from the darkness and seized Pony’s throat.

*Bang!* She was slammed into the prison wall with a crash.

“Kuh! This brat…!”

She tried to cast magic—but her body convulsed with electricity.

“Huh—uh—uh—uh!”

“Shh.”

A man with wild hair stepped forward, pressing a finger to his lips.

Pony’s eyes widened.

“You?!”

Electric Monster Licorn.

“What?”

Licorn smirked, his drunken grin widening.

“Pony? Long time.”

“You bastard! Let go!”

The woman in the cell waved at Licorn, not bothering to cover her naked body.

“Brother! Who is this bitch? Did you bring another woman besides me? When only I—ugh!”

Licorn flicked his hand. The woman’s head exploded with a pop.

*A massive electric shock.*

“Well, you were royalty too. What a shame. But be proud. You’ve grown enough to be seen as a threat.”

Licorn laughed, revealing rotten teeth.

“Die.”

Electricity surged through the hand choking her. Pony’s eyes widened in terror.

*Is that…?!*

Behind Licorn, a figure shimmered in golden light.

*Shirone?*
