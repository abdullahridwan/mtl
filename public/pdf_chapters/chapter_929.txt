“The being standing before us now is the ultimate pinnacle of humanity.”

“Ugh—! Too loud! I don’t care about your zero point, but all this noise is annoying!”

*Crack! Knock!*

Arius clutched his chin, groaning in pain. Kang Nan stepped forward, his expression grim.

“What happened?”

It was a relief that Miro’s mind had been restored—but at the same time, Kang Nan felt a bitter ache. He couldn’t begin to imagine what she’d endured.

“As you can see,” Miro said, smiling faintly.

Not a single trace of trauma remained on her face.

“It’s over.”

Gaold was gone from her mind.

The mind could change in an instant.

But even so, the weight of what she’d survived was unmistakable.

‘I couldn’t have done it,’ Kang Nan thought. ‘Not like this.’

“I feel fine now,” Miro said. “I’ll take care of the world from here. Just watch.”

Countless monks had died, but no scars lingered in Miro’s heart.

“Then shall we continue?”

She rolled up her sleeves and stepped forward. Uriel, who had regained his senses without even realizing it, stood in her path.

“Continue? Do you really think I’ve started something?”

The corner of Miro’s mouth twitched upward.

‘That attack was definitely embarrassing…’

True, she’d counterattacked blindly just before being struck by the Geon of Paradise. But she hadn’t expected the damage to be this minimal.

“Ho ho! You speak boldly. You claim you were beaten by my infinite cheap sphere and lost your mind?”

“Yes,” Uriel said calmly. “I was.”

“Humans do the same, don’t they? You jump when someone shouts behind you, or when you see a disgusting bug.”

Miro didn’t back down.

“Then why did you kneel? I saw it with my own eyes—you stumbled and fell to your knees.”

“That’s…????”

Uriel fell silent.

He had no rebuttal. But he couldn’t understand why he was even arguing.

“It doesn’t matter. If you have something to do, do it. This time, I will destroy you.”

“You’re not normal either.”

“I know you’re ignorant because of Kariel’s symmetry—but aren’t you just as ignorant of angels?”

“What do you mean?”

“I understand you. Destruction is one of the simplest and most powerful ways to solve problems. Humans face countless issues. Say you have a rival. Kariel would devise endless strategies to surpass him. But you? You just eliminate the problem.”

“Kill the rival. The army comes? Eliminate the army. Put someone in jail? Eliminate the jail. Eliminate the accusers, the kings, the nations…

The simplest solution. The most powerful.

Uriel’s planetary form trembled, shaken once more by the Thousand-Armed Guanshi Yin’s earlier assault.

McLean Guffin.
