‘Poor person.’

These were the tears of Nane, a human being, not a Buddha.

“Sorry. This… this…”

“I really don’t understand.”

Her thumb brushed beneath Nane’s eyes.

“It’s so different… yet…”

They were so different.

“But crying… it looks the same.”

The moment Nane saw Amy’s face—her eyes holding the same quiet sorrow as Shirone’s—her heart began to race.

Gaold came to his senses.

“Where am I?”

Life was returning.

Because he was alive.

“Quack! Kuck!”
