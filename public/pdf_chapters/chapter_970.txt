“Wow, that was quick. Perfect timing.”

*That attack was fast—on par with mine. Does that mean there are Schema Masters even among the prisoners?*

“Haha! Of course not.”

To gain 0.1 seconds. To lift 1 kg more. To earn one extra point.

*Negative 7 o’clock… was a child.*
