A pain so vast it defied eternity.

“Ah…????”

Her memories did not return.

But now, she understood what she had lost.

“Guffin.”

Her thoughts could not reach him, yet her heart overflowed with him.

“You left.”

It was as if her entire soul had been ripped away, leaving behind an emptiness vast enough to swallow the universe and still remain hollow.

“It was connected!”

This is the only truth.

I loved you.”

‘I want to know.’

Fragments of memory flashed through her mind, chaotic and unconnected.

“Right now!”

“……!”

But her hand froze on the handle.

“No.”

She didn’t know why, but going to Ankera now would only bring another tragedy.

Even though she felt she had nothing left to lose, that fear remained.

‘I have to wait.’

She collapsed to her knees.

‘Why do I feel this isn’t over?’

Unwilling to fail again, Ikael lifted her head.

“Don’t worry, Guffin. I’ll keep going.”

The erasure of Guffin had carved a void in Ikael’s heart—something no force in existence could fill.

Nineteen years later, Ikael finally reconstructed the full imprint of that memory, restoring it perfectly.

But Ikael was not the only one shattered by Guffin’s disappearance.

‘Find it.’

Tracking the erasure was like searching for a single strand of straw in infinite space.

“Kiyiyi!”

‘Even if the system collapses, this must be found.’

‘Maclein Guffin.’

‘Error found! Restore! Fault found! Restore! Error found!’
