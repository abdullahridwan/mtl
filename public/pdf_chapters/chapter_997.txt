Existence of Absence (2)

~*~

An explosion erupted at the center where Lian stood. The blast was powerful enough to obliterate everything within its radius, yet the allied party behind Lian remained unharmed.

Everyone stared ahead in shock. Shirone, who had arrived without realizing it, was already casting *Hand of God*.

He saw Ymir leaning forward, his palm raised to block the explosion with a wall of light.

“Whoa!”

The entire area turned to black ash—but Ymir still stood, breathing, alive.

‘Did I miss it?’

Just as everyone began to wonder, Mi Le’s chest split open, and blood gushed out.

“Hmm.”

Even as he watched his own blood spill before his eyes, his expression didn’t change.

‘Ymir… … He was cut by the sword.’

It was Lian’s group that was stunned. If there had been an army of heaven, they would have been utterly shocked.

“Outstanding, Lian.”

Shirone, observing Ymir’s condition, could only sincerely acknowledge Lian’s feat.

‘In the history of Omega, Ogent is the only one who has ever wounded Ymir’s true body.’

Now, Lian’s sword had reached that same level.

“Big!”

Smoke rose from where *Idea* had struck, and Lian’s body began to reform.

Ymir watched for a moment, then tightened his chest muscles to stop the bleeding.

“It is standard.”

Shirone adjusted the *Hand of God* in front of him.

“Remember this level of power. I can’t affect Ymir with anything less.”

Even so… …

‘I couldn’t see what he swung or how. This isn’t power a human should be able to produce.’

Lian’s regeneration was slow, but in the realm of divine sword unification, the physical form was secondary.

“I waited beneath the frozen sea. It was boring. I’m not patient, but I haven’t had much fun since I came out.”

It was the years of enduring Ogent’s relentless attacks, strike after strike, endlessly.

“You said you would return. He told me so.”

Ymir looked down at Lian, now fully restored, and nodded.

“You keep your promise, Ozent.”

The origin of the Ogent bloodline—the pride of ancestral legacy—burned within Lian’s heart.

‘But… …

My head feels colder than before.’

‘He avoided it. A full-body strike.’

What about Ymir?

He had also dodged the attack with everything he had.

Hadn’t he?

Lian focused every nerve, yet still couldn’t sense Ymir’s presence.

Shirone spoke.

“Lian, fall back.”

He pulled Lian to the side of the party and placed *Hand of God* between them and Ymir.

“As you’ve already experienced, Ymir’s attacks come in two types of shockwaves. The first is the impact on the photon field—this is physical force.”

“The second is the unique wavelength emitted by his body as he moves. If you’re caught in that wave, your mind will be paralyzed. Prepare for it in advance.”

Shirone’s warning was accurate—Shirone had already felt it—but it still felt overwhelming.

‘Are you saying he has no natural enemies?’

It was said that physics, magic, laws, and even reality itself shattered under a single blow.

“I know you well,” Ymir said, lips pursed.

“It feels familiar. Something passed down long before Ogent.”

*Hand of God* reacted instantly to Ymir’s movement as he twitched his fingers.

“Great.”

Shirone, Blitz, Aetra, Lian, Tess, Amy, Nade, Miro, Zulu, Kang, Nan, Sein, Enox, Garcia.

Then, Ymir raised his hands and flicked his fingers toward the allied forces beyond.

“Come on.”

They felt it for the first time.

‘I haven’t thought about anything until now.’

What was Ymir’s true method when he decided to kill?

“Huh… huh…”

Tess clenched her jaw, suppressing a scream, as Ymir’s body snapped forward.

Muscle vibration.

The shockwave from his body paralyzed their minds, and hallucinations flickered before their eyes.

“First…”

No one moved. Ymir sprang into the maze.

“Shall we start with you?”

Miro, who had been watching the front, shifted his gaze—just in time to catch Ymir’s fist.

Ymir laughed, amused.

“Hoo?” The unshaken look in her eyes revealed how deeply she had penetrated.

Time compressed to its limit.

‘All-sky Projection.’

*Thousand Arms Guanshiyin* struck Ymir from both sides as he raised his arms to guard.

‘Can I survive this?’ Miro braced for a fatal blow—then saw Ymir blink through the defense.

‘There’s no shock.’

Before the eerie sensation could register, Ymir stepped forward and extended his fist.

At that moment, when no one could react, *Hand of God* seized Ymir’s body.

“Huh!”

Shirone’s nose wrinkled.

‘I can’t stop this.’

Even with full quantum signal output, he couldn’t completely suppress Ymir’s strength.

“Kuaaa!”

While still trapped in *Hand of God*, Ymir launched a devastating strike. Miro twisted his head aside.

Bang! Bang! Bang! Bang!

Ymir’s fists generated wind pressure that knocked down buildings behind the maze like dominoes.

“Sigh… Sigh…”

Miro exhaled heavily.

‘I almost died.’

If *Hand of God* hadn’t restrained Ymir, his head would have been blown clean off.

Shirone frowned.

“This is the limit.”

*Hand of God* dissolved into light smoke and wrapped tightly around Ymir’s body.

It would have an effect—but how much, no one could guess.

“Go!”

As Lian kicked off the ground, the mages behind him activated their spells.

At the stage of sword unification, Lian’s body couldn’t withstand the strain and exploded with a bang.

Lian, now in a state of obsession and soul fusion, swung *Idea* at the speed of thought.

The atmosphere detonated.

Through the thick haze, the mages attacked Ymir, guided by the Spirit Zone.

Fire, lightning, wind, and ice exploded in unison, devastating the area around Baska’s walls.

“Whoa.”

Even the fighters were stunned by their own power—but Ymir was merely tickled.

“Hahaha!”

Garcia, pushing her mental strength to the limit, summoned *Hellfire* from the sky.

Ymir, straining briefly to break free from *Hand of God*, bent his legs and loaded his fist.

Amy cried out.

“Danger… …”

Before she could finish, Ymir’s strike shot upward, obliterating the *Hellfire* with a single hook.

“Master!”

Garcia, on the opposite side, collapsed—her upper body gone.

Instant death.

“Aaaaaaa!”

A fire giant erupted from Amy in fury and charged at Ymir.

“Dangerous!”

Shirone followed, and Sein cast *Sun Moonlight Wheel* to maximize *Hand of God*’s restraining power.

“Quack. Kkkkkkkkk.”

Ymir, feeling the damage accumulate, laughed with trembling shoulders.

“Ha ha ha ha!” At the same time, he kicked off the ground and drove his fist into Sein’s face.

*Thousand Arms Guanshiyin* pushed three men aside with its right hand and raised its capital to strike down this Mir.

Ymir bent down, a tear at the corner of his mouth.

“Did you think you would win?”

Because he was polar. Because he was omnipotent. Because he inherited the Ogent lineage. Because he was a fire world. Because he was the world’s strongest magician.

“Did you really think you could win?”

Dodging the follow-up from *Thousand Arms Guanshiyin*, Ymir twisted his waist and pulled back his fist.

*Idea* flew at terrifying speed—but Ymir’s strike landed first.

‘Posmetry.’

Zulu canceled the lich summoning, called forth the space-time bird along with the maze, and soared away in a flash of light.

Aaaaaang!

A shockwave swept the front line. *Idea*’s blade reached Ymir’s neck.

‘What?’

Time slowed infinitely. Lian’s mind saw Ymir spinning rapidly.

How fast was that?

Before he could process it, Ymir’s fist pierced Lian’s psychic body.

‘Gagging!’

Lian’s consciousness was flung far away. *Idea* clattered to the ground.

Though unseen, it was clear—Lian’s mind had missed the great sword.

“Lian!”

Tess screamed and lunged forward—but Shirone blocked her path.

“No, Tess.”

Even Shirone, who had lived through all of Omega’s history, couldn’t comprehend what had just happened.

‘He attacked the psychic body.’

Ymir brushed his knees and straightened up.

“It’s nothing.”

As he clenched his fist, a brilliant golden aura surged into the sky.

“Link.”

Shirone realized—there was far too little information about Lee Mi Le in all of history.

Ymir laughed in disbelief.

“Why? Did you think you could? No, I didn’t even need to.” Shirone’s gaze dropped—but *Idea* remained motionless.

It might be dead.

“Zulu! Zulu!”

At Miro’s cry, they turned—Zulu had collapsed, spitting up blood.

“… … Don’t make a fuss.”

Just like the attack on Lian’s mind, Ymir’s fist had chased through space and time.

“Blitz. Eitra.”

Shirone turned.

“Block it.”

At his command, the two apostles kicked off the ground and leapt forward.

He didn’t transform into a dragon—because Ymir could grow just as large.

“Go ahead.”

Blitz’s body crackled with electricity as he charged. Aetra followed behind.

‘I’m not sure about the Kwonbeop… …’

After taking Aetra’s elbow strike, Ymir staggered, his back bending sideways.

“Haha.”

Excluding the mutation that was the sum of ten billion Gaia, the dragon was the most powerful being in the universe.

‘Nonsense.’

Aetra smiled bitterly.

The most terrifying thing about Ymir was that no one knew the limits of his abilities.

‘He keeps getting stronger.’

Perhaps he wasn’t even using one-tenth of his true power.

‘No—has he ever reached his limit?’

And at the peak of the dragon’s pincer assault, Ymir raised his power once more.

“Ha ha ha ha!”

When Blitz was sent flying out of sight, Shirone drew a dagger from her bosom.

“*Law Slaughter*.”

The most dangerous weapon in existence—one that altered laws and caused murder.

When the wrath of Yahweh was sealed within it, *Dharma Kill* screamed as red energy poured out.

‘Please! Please!’

Shirone took a step forward, eyes locked on Ymir, feeling the weight in her arms.

‘Get out of space!’

As both Blitz and Aetra were thrown back, black flashes of light surged toward Ymir.

Ymir turned his head sharply and caught the blade of *Law Flesh* in his massive hand.

A thud. The sound of metal breaking.

“What is this toy… … ?”

In the next instant, Ymir’s eyes widened. The hand gripping the *Slayer* trembled violently.

“Ack—uh!”

Shirone clenched his fists.

Except for lawless atrocities, nothing could resist *the killing of the law*.

‘It took hold.’

You couldn’t kill Ymir by being hit by a carriage or crushed by a falling rock.

As the dimension began to collapse, space around Ymir warped.

Simultaneously, an infinite law capable of killing him activated—transcending time and space.

“You do cute things.”

Though his words were mocking as he glanced back at Shirone, his expression was deadly serious.

‘This… … It’s definitely dangerous.’

As if drawn from the entire universe, blackened space began closing in on Ymir.
