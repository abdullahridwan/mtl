Valkyrie Command.

At the end of the mining of the Arca Mountains, Dorothy came to Iruki with the payment documents.

“93 percent of projected mining volume. Can you say that this is enough to complete the mission?”

Iruki nodded his head as he signed.

“Great job.”

Dorothy’s lips pouted.

“What? Do you mean just ‘You worked hard’?”

“You know my personality.”

Dorothy thought that Iruki was blunt, but he was by no means cold.

“You’re just fooling yourself. Did you honestly want to say something else? For example, love… …”

“Let’s go get something to eat. What do you want to eat?” Only then did Dorothy realize.

“What are you doing?”

No matter how difficult the situation, he never expressed himself, but today he seemed nervous.

As Iruki turned away without saying a word, Dorothy followed after him and persistently questioned him.

“Why are you doing that? You can tell me. Is it because of the war?”

“No. Never mind… …”

The moment Iruki grabbed the doorknob and opened the door, he heard a woman’s voice.

“I came to see the General Commander!” When Dorothy poked her head out, a group of people were in a commotion at the end of the hallway.

“You! Do you know where you are right now? Do you think a single battalion commander will be able to meet the Valkyrie General Commander at will?”

“You could at least pass on a word! That bastard—no, the General Commander-in-Chief—will definitely see me.”

“Amy?”

Dorothy’s eyes widened.

“What happened? Why is Amy here?”

“I called her.”

Iruki gave a short answer and gestured to Aromi.

“Tell her to come in.”

Aromi knew that Iruki and Amy were friends, but she also had something to protect.

“But, Commander-in-Chief, this is the Valkyrie HQ. It is a violation of discipline for a lower-ranking officer to meet with the General Commander alone without going through due process.”

“Then what legal procedure is required for a lieutenant colonel-level officer to meet with me alone?”

Aromi’s eyes went blank.

“No such law exists.”

Understanding what she wanted to say, Iruki nodded and entered the room.

“Tell her to come in.”

Ten minutes later, Amy knocked on the door and opened the door.

“Amy.”

Dorothy greeted her warmly, but when she saw Amy’s expression, she lowered her hand.

After an awkward moment, Amy straightened her posture and saluted.

“Salute! Lieutenant Colonel Karmis Amy. Requesting an interview with the Valkyrie General Commander.”

Iruki did not return the salute.

“That’s enough. It’s strange.”

And he said to Dorothy.

“I’m out for a minute.”

Dorothy judged that there was nothing good for her to watch here, but that was exactly why she couldn’t leave.

“No. You’re not even going to talk about military business anyway, are you? Then I deserve to hear it too.”

Amy said.

“I don’t care. I won’t talk for long.”

Iruki let out a small sigh.

“Okay, what do you want to say?”

“Do you know?”

Amy took a step forward.

“As of yesterday noon, my position has changed. I am now a training officer at the Capital Defense Command.”

“I know.”

Amy’s eyebrows twitched.

“Did you order it?”

“Yes.”

Her fists were clenched.

“Why? Why?”

“Education in the rear is just as important as fighting in the front. Your troop training ability is outstanding even among the officers… …”

“Iruki.”

Amy asked again in a low voice.

“Why?”

While Dorothy was daunted by the threatening atmosphere, Iruki spat out his answer as if tossing it away.

“You look like you want to hit me.”

“I just want to know the truth. Why did you send me to the rear? Because I am your friend?” “I promise. Honestly, I would never get angry. Is that really the only reason you pulled me off the front?”

Iruki’s mouth opened slowly.

“Yes.”

At the same time as the words fell, Amy punched Iruki in the face.

Dorothy hurriedly supported him as Iruki rolled to the floor with a crash.

“Iruki! Are you okay?”

When she turned her head, an excited Amy was approaching with her fists raised.

“I’m not done yet.”

Dorothy held out her hand.

“Not the head! If you do that, he’ll be a corpse!”

Thinking it made sense, Amy lifted her foot and aimed at Iruki’s abdomen.

Dorothy held out her hand.

“Not here either! This is mine.” Tears welled up in Amy’s eyes.

“… … What do you know?”

Iruki still had a calm expression on his face.

“Do you know how much I trained to get to this point and how many deaths I passed through?”

“I don’t know.”

Iruki straightened his upper body and sat up.

“I don’t need to know that to save you. I made my decision, and I will take responsibility for it. As a junior officer, you will stay quietly in the rear.”

Only then did Dorothy understand the situation.

‘That’s how it happened.’

She understood the shame Amy must have felt, and the anguish of Iruki, who had to make the decision despite knowing it.

Amy stepped back.

“I can’t accept it. I know it’s a waste of time, but that’s why I’m here. Send me back to the front line. You can at least do that.”

“No. I’m a general. If you want to protest, I can send you to the brig instead of the Capital Defense Command.”

“… … Is that what you want? That I ‘survive like a coward alone while my subordinates and comrades are all dying’?”

“Yes.”

“You… … !”

Just as Amy was about to curse, Iruki screamed, his face distorted.

“Just live a little longer!”

It was the first time Dorothy had seen Iruki get angry since his days at magic school.

Iruki jumped up.

“I’m already disturbed, and you’re tormenting me on top of it! Do you know what instructions I gave you? Do you want to know the truth that badly? Then I’ll tell you. If you go there, everyone will die! A 99% chance of death! Do you know what that means? You will surely die if you go there, you idiot!”

Once his emotions had exploded, he couldn’t control them.

“So what if I’m a coward! I took responsibility for everything by sending you off! What, are you grateful to have a military friend? Then what? Even if you feel ashamed, bear with it! I said I’ll take care of everything if you just keep your mouth shut!”

“I understand your heart. But Iruki… …”

“Shut up! How could you know my heart? Do I look like a madman to you? Death is plainly calculated, and you want me to let you go? Aren’t you thinking of Shirone? If you die—if you die horribly there—do you think I’ll be able to face Shirone? I just want you to live!”

Amy found another face hidden in the face of Iruki, whose eyes were red with rage.

‘He’s not angry.’

Tears ran down Amy’s cheeks.

‘That Iruki… …’

He seemed terrified.

It was the first time she had seen him angry, but it was the first time she had seen him so afraid… … .

‘It’s not a lie.’

If Iruki said she would die, then she really would die.

“I see what you mean. And I fully understand your feelings and your desire to save me.”

Amy couldn’t say for certain that she wouldn’t do the same as Iruki if their positions were reversed.

“But before I am Shirone’s lover, before I am your friend, I am a soldier. That is my own choice, not something defined by anyone else. And a soldier can’t do that.”

Iruki leaned against the desk and lowered his head.

“Did you not hear me? You’ll die if you go. If you’re crazy about wanting to die, then do as you please.”

Amy shook her head.

“I don’t want to die. Of course I want to live. I want to live happily with Shirone and my friends. But you know what? As much as I want to live… …” Amy struggled to hold back her tears.

“Other people will want to live too. Even so, we are fighting to protect humanity.”

Iruki said in a hoarse voice.

“… … Every time I give orders, at least thousands die. Sad news keeps coming. Where 10,000 died, where 100,000 died. Like hallucinations, they linger endlessly.”

It was not a place where one could stay sane.

“It is a terrible thing. Even if you don’t want to imagine what it’s like for 100,000 people to die, it appears in your dreams. But no imagination… …”

Iruki covered his head.

“It cannot be compared to your death. Every time I think of a face I know, a look I know, a pain I know, I feel like going crazy.”

“Iruki.”

Amy smiled sadly as she approached Iruki and stroked his cheeks.

Iruki raised his head with tears in his eyes.

“Once you get there, I can’t help you under any circumstances. It scares me so much.”

Amy said with a pitying look.

“Iruki… …”

My dear friend.

Trapped completely alone in that secret room of gray matter, how much must you have thought and worried?

‘Because he’s more level-headed than anyone else.’

The current situation was unbearably frightening to him.

Dorothy wiped away her tears.

‘Amy, can’t you just stay here? Since it’s come to this, can’t we just live?’

The words surged up in her throat, but they were useless in the face of Amy’s convictions.

Amy said firmly.

“Do not worry about it. I can overcome any situation. I am a soldier. There is nothing scary in the world.”

She didn’t say she would live.

“Send me back to the battlefield. My subordinates are as important to me as I am to you. I don’t want to send them to their deaths while I alone am guaranteed survival. Do you understand?”

“You are really stubborn.”

“Haha.”

Amy, who felt she had heard his answer, slowly stepped back and saluted.

“I will return to the unit.” Even though she was going to her death, her eyes were more alive than when she had first entered, and Iruki let out a sigh.

“… … Approve.”

Only then did Amy open the door with a satisfied smile and waved at the two of them.

“I’m sorry for coming in all of a sudden. I’m too busy with work, so I’ll go first. Take care.”

She had to catch up diligently from now on so that she could keep pace with the unit’s rapid march.

“Amy… …”

Dorothy tried to say something, but Amy had already closed the door and left.

“She doesn’t leave any room for emotion.”

After going around the table, Iruki sat down on a chair and buried his face in his hands.

“Iruki, are you okay?”

“How could I be okay? I knew it anyway—she’s had that tyrannical personality since her school days.”

The reason Dorothy couldn’t get closer was because Iruki’s shoulders were shaking.

“Damn it!”

He had thought he could do anything.

‘I’m sorry, Shirone. I couldn’t stop her. Even if it meant being beaten to death, I should have stopped her… …’

That was what he had thought at first.

No matter what criticism he received, even if it meant never seeing Amy again for the rest of his life, he had been determined to persevere.

“I couldn’t stop her!”

He couldn’t break her convictions.

“Sorry. I’m sorry, Shirone!”

Dorothy affectionately hugged Iruki’s shoulders as he burst into tears.

“Get out of the way! Damn it!”

With the approval of the Valkyrie Command, Amy ran toward the space movement magic circle.

Although time was tight, it wasn’t a distance she couldn’t make up if she caught up diligently.

‘Wait for me, you guys. I’m coming.’ The faces of the members who would come out to welcome her shimmered in her mind, and the unit’s morale would soar.

Amy’s eyes sparkled with life.

“Never give up.”

Even if death awaited her, she would not succumb to fear.
