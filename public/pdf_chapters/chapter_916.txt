Valkyrie Command.

At the end of the mining of the Arca Mountains, Dorothy came to Iruki with the payment documents.

“93 percent of projected mining volume. Can you say that this is enough to complete the mission?”

Iruki nodded his head as he signed.

“Great job.”

Dorothy’s lips pouted.

“What? Do you mean ‘You worked hard’?”

“You know my personality.”

Dorothy thought that Iruki was blunt, but he was by no means cold.

“You tend to fool yourself. Did you honestly want to say something else? For example, love… …”

“Let’s go get something to eat. What do you want to eat?” Only then did Dorothy realize.

“What are you doing?”

No matter how difficult the situation, he never expressed himself, but today he seemed nervous.

As Iruki turned around without saying a word, Dorothy followed after him and persistently questioned him.

“Why are you doing that? You can tell me. Is it because of the war?”

“No. Never mind… …”

The moment Iruki grabbed the doorknob and opened the door, he heard a woman’s voice.

“I came to see the General Commander!” When Dorothy poked her head out, a group of people were in a commotion at the end of the hallway.

“You! Do you know where this is? Do you think a single battalion commander will be able to meet the Valkyrie General Commander at will?”

“You can at least go and tell him! That bastard—no, the General Commander—will definitely see me.”

“Amy?”

Dorothy’s eyes widened.

“What happened? Why is Amy here?”

“I called her.”

Iruki gave a short answer and gestured to Aromi.

“Tell her to come in.”

Aromi knew that Iruki and Amy were friends, but she also had something to protect.

“But, General Commander, this is the Valkyrie HQ. It is a violation of discipline for the lower end of the chain of command to meet the General Commander alone without going through due process.”

“Then what legal procedure is needed for a lieutenant colonel-level officer to see me alone?”

Power entered Aromi’s eyes.

“No such law exists.”

Understanding what she wanted to say, Iruki nodded and entered the room.

“Tell her to come in.”

Ten minutes later, Amy knocked on the door and opened the door.

“Amy.”

Dorothy greeted her warmly, but when she saw Amy’s expression, she lowered her hand.

After an awkward moment, Amy straightened her posture and saluted.

“Salute! Lieutenant Colonel Karmis Amy. Requesting an interview with the Valkyrie General Commander.”

Iruki did not return the salute.

“It’s okay, it’s strange.”

And he said to Dorothy.

“I’m out for a minute.”

Iruki had judged that there was nothing good for her to watch, but that was exactly why Dorothy couldn’t leave.

“No. You’re not even talking about the military anyway, are you? Then I deserve to hear it too.”

Amy said.

“I don’t mind. I won’t talk too long.”

Iruki let out a small sigh.

“Okay, what do you want to say?”

“You know, right?”

Amy took a step forward.

“As of yesterday noon, my position has changed. As a training officer at the Capital Defense Command.”

“I know.”

Amy’s eyebrows twitched.

“Did you order it?”

“Yes.”

Her fists were clenched.

“Why? Why?”

“Education in the rear is just as important as fighting in the front. Your troop training ability is outstanding even among officers… …”

“Iruki.”

Amy asked again in a low voice.

“Why?”

While Dorothy was daunted by the threatening atmosphere, Iruki spat it out as if tossing it.

“You look like you want to hit me.”

“I just want to know the truth. Why did you send me to the rear? Because I’m your friend?” “I promise. Tell me honestly—I’ll never get angry. Is that really the only reason you pulled me off the front?”

Iruki’s mouth opened slowly.

“Yes.”

The moment the words fell, Amy punched Iruki in the face.

Dorothy hurriedly supported him as Iruki rolled on the floor with a crash.

“Iruki! Are you okay?”

When she turned her head, an excited Amy was approaching with her fists raised.

“I’m not done yet.”

Dorothy held out her hand.

“Not the head! Hit him there and he’s a corpse!”

Thinking it made sense, Amy lifted her foot and aimed at Iruki’s abdomen.

Dorothy held out her hand.

“Not even here! This is mine.” Tears welled up in Amy’s eyes.

“… … What do you know?”

Iruki still had a calm expression on his face.

“Do you know how much I trained to get to this point and how many deaths I passed through?”

“I don’t know.”

Iruki straightened his upper body and sat down.

“I don’t need to know that to save you. I made my decision and I will take responsibility. A junior officer stays quietly in the rear.”

Only then did Dorothy understand the situation.

‘That’s how it happened.’

She understood the shame Amy must have felt and the anguish of Iruki, who had to make a decision despite knowing it.

Amy stepped back.

“I can’t accept it. I know it’s a waste of time, but that’s why I’m here. Send me back to the front line. That’s all you have to do.”

“No. I’m a general. If you want to protest, I can send you to the brig instead of the Capital Defense Command.”

“… … Is that what you want? ‘My subordinates and comrades are all dying, but I alone survive like a coward’?”

“Yes.”

“You… … !”

Just as Amy was about to swear, Iruki screamed with a distorted face.

“Please, just live!”

It was the first time she had seen Iruki get angry since his days at magic school.

Iruki jumped up.

“I’m already disturbed as it is, and now you’re tormenting me too! Do you know what instructions I gave you? Do you want to know the truth that badly? Then I’ll tell you. If you go there, everyone will die! A 99% chance of death! Do you know what I mean? You will surely die if you go there, you idiot!”

Once his emotions exploded, he couldn’t control them.

“So what if that’s cowardly? I took responsibility for everything to get you out! You should be grateful to have a friend in the military! So what then? Even if you feel ashamed, bear with it! I said I’d take care of everything if you just kept your mouth shut!”

“I understand your heart. But Iruki… …”

“Shut up! How could you know my heart? Do I look like a madman to you? Death is plainly calculated—do you still ask me to let you go?”

“Don’t you think of Shirone? If you die, if you die horribly there, do you think I’ll be able to face Shirone? I just want you to live!”

Amy found another face hidden in Iruki’s face, flushed red with rage.

‘He’s not angry.’

Tears ran down Amy’s cheeks.

‘That Iruki… …’

He seemed terrified.

It was the first time he had gotten angry, but it was the first time he had been so afraid… … .

‘It’s not a lie.’

If Iruki said she would die, she really would die.

“I see what you mean. And I fully understand your feelings and your desire to save me.”

Amy couldn’t say for sure that she wouldn’t do the same as Iruki if she were in the opposite position.

“But before I am Shirone’s lover, before I am your friend, I am a soldier. That is my own, not something defined by anyone else. And a soldier can’t do that.”

Iruki leaned against the desk and lowered his head.

“Did you not hear me? You’ll die if you go. If you’re crazy about wanting to die, you can do as you please.”

Amy shook her head.

“I don’t want to die. Of course I want to live. I want to live happily with Shirone and my friends. But you know what? As much as I want to live… …” She struggled to hold back her tears.

“Other people will want to live too. Even so, we are fighting to protect humanity.”

Iruki said in a hoarse voice.

“… … Every time I give orders, at least thousands die. Sad news keeps coming. Where 10,000 died, where 100,000 died. Like a hallucination, it lingers endlessly.”

It was not a place where you could stay sane.

“It is a terrible thing. Even if you don’t want to imagine what it’s like for 100,000 to die, it appears in your dreams. But no imagination… …”

Iruki covered his head.

“It cannot be compared to your death. Every time I think of a face I know, a look I know, a pain I know, I feel like going crazy.”

“Iruki.”

Amy smiled sadly as she approached Iruki and stroked his cheeks.

Iruki raised his head with tears in his eyes.

“Once you get there, I can’t help you under any circumstances. It scares me so much.”

Amy said with a pitiful look.

“Iruki… …”

My dear friend.

Trapped in a completely lonely secret room of gray matter, how much did you think and worry?

‘Because he was cooler-headed than anyone else.’

The current situation was unbearably frightening.

Dorothy wiped away her tears.

‘Amy, can’t we just stay here? Since it happened like this, can’t we just live?’

The words rose to her throat, but she was just a fool in front of Amy’s beliefs.

Amy said firmly.

“Do not worry about it. I can overcome any situation. I am a soldier. There is nothing scary in the world.”

She didn’t say she would live.

“Send me back to the battlefield. My subordinates are as important to me as I am to you. I don’t want to send them to their deaths while I alone am guaranteed to survive. You understand?”

“You are really stubborn.”

“Haha.”

Amy, who thought she had heard the answer, slowly stepped back and saluted.

“I will return to the unit.” Even though she was going to her death, her eyes were more alive than when she had first entered, and Iruki let out a sigh.

“… … Approve.”

Only then did Amy open the door with a satisfied smile and waved at the two of them.

“I’m sorry for coming in all of a sudden. I’m too busy with work, so I’ll go first. Have a good time.”

She had to diligently catch up from now on so that she could keep pace with the rapid march of the unit.

“Amy… …”

Dorothy tried to say something, but Amy had already closed the door and left.

“She didn’t want to leave room for emotion.”

After going around the table, Iruki sat down on a chair and buried his face in his hands.

“Iruki, are you okay?”

“How could I be okay? Anyway, I should know by now that you’ve had that tyrannical personality since your school days.”

The reason Dorothy couldn’t get closer was because Iruki’s shoulders were shaking.

“Damn it… …”

He had thought he could do anything.

‘I’m sorry, Shirone. I couldn’t stop her. Even if I was beaten to death, I should have stopped her… …’

He had thought so at first.

No matter what kind of criticism he received, even if it meant not seeing Amy for the rest of his life, he had been determined to persevere.

“I couldn’t stop her!”

He couldn’t break her convictions.

“Sorry. I’m sorry, Shirone!”

Dorothy affectionately hugged the shoulders of Iruki, who was bursting into tears.

“Get out of the way! Go safely!”

With the approval of the Valkyrie Command, Amy ran toward the space movement magic circle.

Although the time was tight, it wasn’t a distance where she couldn’t join them if she diligently caught up.

‘Wait for me, you guys. I’m coming.’ The faces of the members who would welcome her shimmered in her mind, and the unit’s morale would soar.

Amy’s eyes sparkled with life.

“Never give up.”

Even if death awaited her, she would not succumb to fear.
