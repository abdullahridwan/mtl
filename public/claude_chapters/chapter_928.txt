Omega (2)

~*~

Zion.

Antarctica had taken the full force of Uriel’s Ragnarok, and the continent of ice kept cracking and tearing apart.

As the monks’ temple collapsed, Arius scrambled out of it.

“Keong! Grrr!”

He couldn’t see it yet, but he could already feel the presence in the air.

“The heavenly army!”

The monks guarding Zion moved at once, but there was nothing they could do.

“The author…”

A majestic pressure spread out from Uriel as he flew through the sky.

*An archangel. And now, of all times…*

Miro and most of the other leaders were away. There was no way to stop an archangel.

“Humans.”

The giant landed with a heavy thud, and the monks each put their hands together in a sign.

“Hm.”

Uriel looked around. The ice was melting in the extreme heat, and steam rose on every side.

“Where is Miro?”

Dharma of the Eternal Community, who had joined Zion, walked toward Uriel to speak for everyone.

Behind Dharma stood Hyega, Dharma’s disciple, and Sky Yugo, who had once been a candidate for the Ivory Tower.

“Dharma.”

Archangels and their subjects were worlds apart in rank, but Dharma and Hyega of the Eternal Community knew Uriel as well.

“Are you betraying me?”

“A seeker is someone who goes forward in search of what is right. Those left behind will always feel betrayed…”

Fighting spirit rose from Dharma’s body.

“That is why we will not grab hold of a rotten rope.”

As Uriel listened calmly, he felt the force of the Law tightening around him from all sides.

At the same moment, Dharma thrust out a fist.

*Pungryu Geumgangwon!*

The air compressed, and a blast of wind shaped like a fist slammed into Uriel.

“Now!”

Uriel’s body was knocked back dozens of meters, and every monk poured out the Law at full strength.

“Redeemer!”

Angels born from concepts have a weakness: the Law of Opposition.

That was exactly why Ikael had ordered Zion to be struck first when the heavenly army reached this planet.

“Ridiculous.”

Even the Law that sealed away the psychic realm was not enough to block the concept that was Uriel.

*No. It has always been this way.*

Adrias Miro had once said so.

*Isn’t humanity a race that has endured by squeezing out every last drop of a single life?*

Each angel had a different opinion, but Miro was the only human Uriel acknowledged.

“Destroy it.”

Uriel’s celestial sword wheel formed beyond his elbow and spun at a terrifying speed.

*Hiiiiiing!*

The shriek of the air alone turned the monks pale.

“Chun Bo-ryun!”

As the black disk flew, Hyega threw out one arm.

*Keumgangseong!*

Her body turned clear, and the air around her hardened into diamond forty meters thick.

“Gah!”

The moment the Chun Bo-ryun struck it, Hyega’s focus shook badly.

*I can’t hold it.*

The defensive wall tilted. The Chun Bo-ryun began to climb it, grinding against the surface.

“Whoa!”

It bounced off, but a moment later the huge diamond cracked with a screech.

“This is…”

Hyega watched the crack run toward her, and her eyes filled with resignation.

“Hyega! Get out of the way!”

The moment Dharma’s cry ended, Hyega’s diamond body shattered into pieces.

“Hyega!”

Eyes bloodshot, Dharma charged at Uriel, throwing golden fists one after another.

*Bang! Bang! Bang! Bang! Bang!*

As Uriel knocked the golden fists aside with the Gon of Paradise, Sky Yugo attacked from the side.

The incarnation of a crusader in white armor released cause and effect all at once, and three swords flew in from different directions.

“Tch…”

Uriel’s voice was displeased.

Hearing an ominous vibration, Yugo saw a huge judicial halo fill his vision.

*Ragnarok.*

White electricity wrapped around Uriel’s body, and thunder broke loose.

*Kwaaang!*

Even Dharma, driven by duty since Hyega’s death, stopped in place.

“Unbelievable…”

Yugo was gone without a trace, and the ice continent evaporated wherever Uriel moved.

*Is the gap really this big?*

Dharma had known nothing about archangels, but even among archangels, Uriel was a fighting force on another level.

*So this is Uriel.*

The rumor that his strength was equal to the archangel Ikael’s was no exaggeration.

Dharma asked, “What do you want?”

“Destruction.”

Uriel pointed at the monks.

“Those who betray God will meet disaster.”

“He is not our god.”

“There is only one God.”

Uriel’s logic was utterly simple. There was no way to persuade him.

“I will fight.”

Behind Dharma, the monks steeled their fighting spirit.

“Humans really are strange.”

It was a question Uriel had carried ever since Miro blocked the heavenly army with a space-time barrier.

“Will you fight a battle you cannot win?”

“You don’t understand…”

Dharma bent both knees.

“In human life, there is no battle that can be won.”

Uriel watched as all the monks, Dharma among them, rushed toward him.

“Is that so.”

The face of Miro, holding back the heavenly army, came to his mind.

“Is that why you fight?”

Uriel’s face twisted as if he resented something, and tears ran down it.

“If so.”

The monks screamed with all their strength.

“Aaaaaaaaah!”

As the judicial halo Ragnarok turned, Uriel moved.

“How pitiful.”

White electricity shot up from the ground and pierced the gray clouds across dozens of kilometers.

“There!”

Riding Kydra, Zulu’s beast, Miro pointed at the lightning rising from the center of Antarctica.

“Oh my god…”

Kang Nan’s mouth fell open.

They hadn’t even reached land yet, and the lightning was clearly visible.

*From the size of it, it’s already over.*

If the heavenly army had taken Zion, charging in like this would be suicide.

“Miro, we should change the plan…”

“No!”

Miro cut Kang Nan off.

“Someone will be alive.”

Even if not everyone, if there was a chance that even one person had survived, she could not give up.

“I’ll go faster.”

Zulu’s pupils rolled back into darkness, and Kydra groaned.

*Kyaaaaaaaa!*

They tore into Antarctica on a fierce wind, but the continent had already been ripped to pieces.

*Even for an archangel, this kind of power is impossible.*

Miro had a feeling she knew who it was.

“Uriel.”

By the time they reached Zion, Zulu, drenched in sweat, had his eyes rolled up.

“Zulu!”

The moment Kang Nan caught the unconscious Zulu, Kydra vanished into the air.

“Ugh!”

Thrown at the ground by the tremendous momentum, Kang Nan braked by tensing both thighs, slid more than two kilometers across the ice, and twisted around in a hurry.

With a jolt, Kang Nan came to a stop and looked out over the destruction of Zion.

The bodies of the monks lay torn apart on the blood-soaked ice.

“So you’ve come.”

“Crunch! Crunch!”

Arius, the only survivor, was writhing with Uriel’s foot crushing his chest.

“Uriel.”

“So you are the woman Heaven has been talking about.”

He was surprised, because he had been curious about the relationship between Gaold, Miro, and Kang Nan.

“Have you made peace, then?”

“No. It’s a temporary alliance.”

Miro walked past Kang Nan.

“Humans don’t change that easily.”

“Kreung! Kreurreung!”

When his master appeared, Arius struggled and bared his ferocious teeth, and Uriel lifted his foot.

“Kong! Kung!”

Arius immediately turned around and rubbed his cheek against Miro’s leg, and she crouched down.

“I told you to look after this place. What happened?”

Arius spoke.

“Run.”

They were the first human words he had spoken in a long time, but Miro only smiled.

“He is not an opponent you can beat.”

Arius knew, because he was the only one who had ever explored the labyrinth inside her.

*A crack in my heart.*

Her feelings for Gaold had pushed through that crack, widened it into a huge rift, and now they ruled her.

“It’s fine.”

Gaold had once said:

“It just means the time has come to make a choice.”

As she walked toward Uriel, only ten steps away, Miro put her life in order.

Most of it was easy, but one stubborn question remained.

*A crack in the heart.*

*Why did it look like that to me?*

*I thought I hated men like him.*

If there had been no crack in the first place, the feeling would never have grown so large.

“Miro, stop.”

At Kang Nan’s words, she stopped walking, and before she knew it, Uriel was standing right in front of her.

“I’m sorry, I haven’t finished sorting things out. Could you give me a little more time?”

One of Kang Nan’s eyebrows went up.

“Sorting things out? In this situation…”

“There is no need.”

Cutting the words off, Uriel indifferently raised the Gon of Paradise.

“Everything will be destroyed anyway.”

Once destroyed, neither interest nor existence would mean anything.

“Sigh. That’s a shame.”

Looking up at the Gon of Paradise, alive with power, Miro felt nervous for the first time in her life.

*I just want to sort this out. So why is the crack still there?*

“Dodge it, you idiot!”

As the Gon of Paradise spun, burning the air, Kang Nan screamed and kicked off the ground.

Miro sighed.

“Haa.”

In the end, she had to admit it.

“Yes. You were right.”

Even as death came for her, Miro turned her thoughts to Gaold.

“You…”

Before Kang Nan could finish:

“Gaold.”

Miro smiled brightly.

“The truth is, I like you.”

And now:

*Goodbye.*

As she closed her eyes, the blow came down toward the crown of Miro’s head, and for Kang Nan, time seemed to slow.

*It’s closing.*

The heart that had shattered as it fell was healing cleanly.

*Ahh…*

Arius could picture it without seeing.

Such a beautiful birth.

*Erase it.*

For Miro, it was a pain as great as the whole world.

*I’m sorry, Gaold.*

What pain it was.

A feeling so terrible that she couldn’t think straight even for a moment clawed at her heart.

*Don’t forgive me.*

When she suddenly opened her eyes, tears of blood ran down Miro’s cheeks.

The Gon of Paradise, which had singed the ends of her hair on its way past, came back around once more.

*Incarnation art.*

Thousands of arms sprouted from behind Miro’s back and caught the Gon of Paradise.

“What?”

The Gon of Paradise stopped dead without any warning, and the air burst outward in every direction.

*She caught the Gon of Paradise?*

The hairline cracks that had been etched into Miro’s heart were wiped away like wind.

Thousand Arms Guanyin, Projection Across the Whole Sky. Tens of thousands of arms burst out from Miro’s sides as she turned toward Uriel.

With perfect vision, Miro’s eyes flashed, and the palms spread out to her left and right struck Uriel one after another.

The endless attacks came in a single instant, shook Uriel, and then hit him with one final blow.

“Ugh!”

Knocked aside, Uriel dug into the ground and caught his balance.

*Her spirit…*

The asteroid floating above his head couldn’t absorb the shock and shook violently.

“Gah!”

When Uriel dropped to one knee and raised his head, Miro was brushing off her hands as she walked toward him.

“Phew. Is it because it’s been a while?”

The tears of blood had already dried on her cheeks. Miro smiled arrogantly.

“My aim is off.”

Time would take care of that anyway.
