“Is it starting?”

Electricity flickered in Blitz’s eyes as he looked down on the Alpones Mountains from the sky.

Through the shared network, he could hear the thoughts of Karatorsa, the Mudeungryong.

*Dragons.*

The language of dragons is binary.

*The smallest unit of information that can make up a form. That is why it is exact.*

In human language, the word “red” covers a spectrum with almost infinite shades. In binary, every possible red in the universe can be stated exactly.

*Nothing is lost when we speak to each other.*

There is no endless chain of interpretation. That was how the Balaur had been able to build a vast database and share their thoughts with one another.

*But even so, that is only reading information.*

The Ultima System recorded in the Balaur database was something on another level.

*One digit.*

The Ultima System could take in every signal in this world on its own.

*In a world taken completely apart, what you face is probably emptiness.*

Nothing in it truly exists as information.

*And you have the one thing that can live in that empty void.*

The heart.

Where there is nothing, only the mind flows.

*That is what humans are.*

Sooner or later, Shirone would understand it.

*Only humans can go beyond the administrator’s authority and strike at the world outside.*

That was possible only when every mind and every quantum signal was unified into one.

*The Gaians were like that.*

The Ultima System against the Akashic Records.

“This is something you should see too.”

Blitz’s gaze pierced the illusion over the Alpones Mountains and reached deep underground.

Wrapped in Karatorsa’s enormous light, Shirone stopped thinking and spoke.

“Do you mean all the information in the world? Omniscience?”

Shirone’s Ultima System took in Karatorsa’s binary without any difficulty.

“It is not like the academic knowledge humans define. That kind of knowledge is, in the end, a matter of interpretation. What I want to give you is what exists on its own. That is knowledge.”

The light pressed down on Shirone as if it had weight.

“I am Karatorsa, the guardian of time. Without time, there is no phenomenon. Nothing can heat up, melt, or freeze.”

That was why it had been indifferent.

“Only I can lay out, moment by moment, the fleeting signals that make up this world. Now I will give you the record. The whole world, from the beginning of time to its end.”

A powerful voice filled him.

“Omniscience. Omega.”

Knowledge is held within the flow of time.

*The history of everything.*

Shirone remembered the day he completed the spine of knowledge in the great library of the Ogent family.

*The way of the wizard.*

That was where it had all begun.

And now, that seeker of truth was trying to take in everything in this world.

“I accept it.”

An endless flood of data began to soak into Shirone’s light.

“It will be a long journey.”

All the way back to the beginning of the universe.

As he returned to that point, Shirone’s sense of self faded little by little until it disappeared completely.

“Ahh…”

Everything was pouring in.

In the beginning, there was light.

*Huff!*

A flash exploded across Shirone’s vision, and he opened his eyes in a shock he couldn’t describe.

*I…*

Karatorsa’s record laid out binary information without end.

*Ankera?*

With his ten senses, which went beyond time and space, Shirone took in the whole of this world.

*What is this?*

The first thing Ankera felt, on realizing it existed, was the question of cause and effect.

*Did I have a dream?*

Was it possible that it had simply come into existence out of nothing?

*Or is this the dream?*

Ankera agonized in a world that didn’t even have time yet, and finally reached a conclusion.

“I cannot know.”

Some world might really have existed before, or this might be nothing but an illusion with no one to have it.

“It is all that I perceive.”

Mind Program: God.

Ankera, the first to perceive the world, spread its mind through quantum signals.

Eight concepts separated out of the unified field, and the universe took shape according to the speed of time.

The sun and countless planets began to move according to their own rules, driven by the first force.

*The beginning of the law.*

No human mind could predict it, but from that moment on, cause and effect were already set.

Following the law of causality, something that could be called life appeared in the Goldilocks zone.

*Cells.*

The simplest organic matter went through countless trials, repeating evolution and selection.

The environment was still harsh.

“Taeseong.”

Argones, the father of all creatures, slowly rose out of the boiling sea onto land.

“The strongest life form has been born.”

After saying that, Argones went still. By the time he spoke again, an enormous amount of time had passed.

“It is a mutation.”

Taeseong turned to look, and there in the sky flew the strongest creature Shirone knew.

*Karatorsa.*

By then, many dragons, including the Mudeungryong, ruled the land, the sea, and the air.

Taeseong said, “They are poor children.”

Argones agreed.

“In time, the environment will stabilize. If that species is preserved, it will destroy the balance of the living world.”

They were a kind of prototype.

“If we act now, while we still can…”

As Argones roused the life in his body, Taeseong looked back at him with a sad expression.

“I think every life is precious.”

“Is that your own opinion? That’s unusual.”

They had both been born of the same Ankera, so it was strange that they thought so differently.

“These are my children. It is all my fault. If I had made a better environment…”

“The law doesn’t care about that.”

Argones paused.

“The world is in its infancy. If it tilts to one side now, the swing will grow beyond control as time goes on. I exist for the living world as a whole, not for any one kind of creature.”

Shirone realized that this was where the conflict between Argones and Gaia had begun.

*But…*

Even in the era Shirone lived in, dragons clearly still existed, as the apostles of time.

*What happened?*

Omega flowed again.

Omniscience was being completed as every event across time and space poured in.

*Roooar!*

The dragon’s roar came to him in binary and became real in Shirone’s senses.

Dozens of shattered moons scattered their fragments across the sky, and a meteor was falling.

“Why…?”

Pinned to the ground, Karatorsa staggered and slowly lifted its head.

“Are you trying to wipe us out?”

“Because you are strong.”

Argones’ voice was cold.

“There is no fixed limit on what a creature may become, but you have grown too strong. You may take that as an honor.”

At first, they had been nothing more than a strong species.

But the tremendous cognitive ability born of that extreme environment kept turning back on itself, deeper and deeper, until…

*The source of the world.*

They had reached the point of ruling over phenomena themselves.

“How can that be a reason for extinction?”

“I do not know.”

Not even Argones knew.

“We exist for no reason. All history is carried out by a mission that comes from beyond this world.”

“Do you mean the world outside?”

Ankera had defined everything in this world, but there was one thing it did not know.

*Where did I come from?*

“Living things inherit the genes of their ancestors. But Ankera is different. When a being is born out of nothing, can you imagine what it thinks? It has only a mission. The mission to fill this world with life.”

“What if that mission changes someday?”

Argones tilted his head and answered after another enormous pause.

“However the whole changes, it is still the whole.”

“Then what do living things mean? Only Ankera can dream a new dream. If every creature is reset, then what are we doing right now?”

“…What are you trying to say?”

“I am talking about your authority. If that day comes, you, as the one in charge of all living things, cannot say you bear no blame.”

Ankera, Gaia, and Argones had each been born with a mission to manage the world.

*My mission…*

Karatorsa spoke.

“I will protect it.”

Argones raised his head.

“What?”

“Even if Ankera dreams a new dream, I will protect the dreams of all living things. I will become the apostle of time…”

Karatorsa raised its enormous body and looked down at Argones, with the other dragons behind it.

“History will not change.”

Argones fell into thought.

*A truly strange creature. A mutant? But even that is nothing more than cause and effect under the law.*

He wanted to believe there had to be a reason for its existence.

“I will be watching.”

Argones allowed the dragons to survive. As he turned and walked away, Taeseong appeared.

“What is it?”

“I’m glad I could protect the children.”

Taeseong smiled and added, “Honey.”

“…Huh.”

Argones walked past with a cold reply, and Taeseong turned and called after him.

“Come and see me often.”

That was the kind of world it was.

An era when they believed they were everything, without knowing the true meaning of their missions.

Shirone kept taking in Omega without end.

And at some point, his ten senses would feel an event that shook the foundation of this world.

“It feels good.”

The environment had stabilized. Argones and Taeseong looked down at the creatures on the ground.

“Yes. They resemble you.”

Like people born from the earth itself, the men had rough skin, while the women’s skin was smooth like wet clay.

All of them had sharp features, and their metallic hair, which fell to their waists, gave them a striking look.

“Shall we call them Gaia?”

At Argones’ words, Taeseong turned her head.

“Can we really?”

“Their form resembles me, but everything about them comes from you. It is better to call them Gaia.”

Taeseong was happy.

“Our children…”

At that moment, Argones’ eyes widened, and a grave light came into them.

“What is it?”

Taeseong felt it too and looked up at the sky, and Shirone looked up with her.

*That isn’t the sky.*

Countless beams of light, coming from somewhere outside the world, were striking the Gaians.

[Enter user code. Link.]

The Gaians who received the signal raised their heads, and golden electricity shone in their eyes.

“Ugh!”

A man frowned, then quickly composed himself and looked around.

“Is this it? It’s terribly empty. That won’t be much fun.”

A woman who looked like a wolf came up to him, checking one hand and then the other.

“We’re the first ones here. Once the missing link code kicks in, I’ll forget everything anyway, but it still feels a bit strange. Usually it finishes while you’re still in the womb.”

The woman’s expression turned hazy. Around them, the other users were already losing their memories.

“Ah… it’s happening to me too.”

The light in her eyes was going out.

“Take care. See you in the next life.”

The woman’s memories were sealed away. The man looked up at the sky and waited for his turn.

“…The beginning of an endless cycle of rebirth.”

Argones arrived.

“What are you?”

The man turned his head and smiled, and Argones was struck with pure shock.

No being in this world, it seemed, could show such complete arrogance.

*The light in those eyes.*

Where did that light come from, the light that looked down on everything in the world?

It was as if he were saying:

*I am your god.*
