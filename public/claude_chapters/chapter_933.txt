Chapter 933

Face to Face (3)

~*~

“Time remaining until purification: 65,400 gyeong hours.”

Nearly an eternity had passed since Guffin fell into Hell.

*That is a truly enormous amount of time.*

Shirone was shaken.

It was a mercy that Omega ran at a thousandth of the speed. If he had lived through Guffin’s time at the same rate, he would have gone mad.

Guffin murmured:

“Time flies.”

His body had wasted away, and his face was so haggard that his eyes were sunken.

But his mind was still intact, and that alone gave Shirone goosebumps.

*Actually, it isn’t that six hundred gyeong hours have really passed.*

Time is a matter of the senses, and Hell is a place where the senses work at their most extreme.

*Intense sensation speeds up the passage of time.*

In other words, the more intense Guffin’s pain, the shorter his purification.

*That is the nature of time.*

Shirone remembered breaking a single moment down into infinity while he was invincible.

The strange signals he had seen when he dug past the 0.666 seconds in every second.

*Maybe those are special signals that act on how time relates to the senses.*

That was the truth about time.

*In the end, what the makers of this world want is efficient purification of data, not punishment for evil.*

Shirone closed his eyes in sadness.

*Is God indifferent?*

Carrying the same question, Guffin reached the end of Hell, where a black wall blocked the way.

If you stared at it, you got the illusion that the darkness on the wall was alive and moving.

“The end of space.”

It was the beginning of the world of nothingness. It looked like a wall, but in truth there was nothing there.

It was also the gate that the emotions of the other world passed through to reach the end, once they had been purified.

*Ogent Guy.*

Shirone could guess what end Lian’s eldest brother had met after he handed over the link and disappeared.

*He went to the world of eternal nothingness, but maybe it isn’t as terrible as I thought.*

After all, Jin Seong-eum would be wandering in Hell without even a chance at purification.

“The infinite wall.”

Guffin turned at a voice behind him. An angel with wings black as shadow was walking toward him.

Recognizing Lucifer, the lord of Hell, Guffin raised a hand and smiled for the first time in a long while.

“Did you come to see me off?”

Maybe because they had fought fiercely for more than six hundred gyeong hours, they no longer felt like strangers.

But Lucifer ignored the joke and turned his gaze to the infinite wall.

“Why are you so impatient? Endure another sixty-five thousand, and you will be purified.”

“I don’t want to stay here even a moment longer.”

That was understandable.

But it wasn’t something you’d expect from a Guffin who had calmly endured six hundred gyeong hours.

Lucifer asked:

“Are you still holding on to false hope?”

After falling into Hell, Guffin had looked for a way back to the real world, but in the end it had been impossible.

Unlike someone merely passing through, one being purified could not escape the other world.

*Because that is how the world was made.*

Guffin looked back at the infinite wall.

“Hope is a good thing. If I break through the realm of nothingness, I’ll be able to return to the real world. Then Hell will have to go on strike.”

From Lucifer’s point of view, it was insane.

“Why are you so obsessed with reality? Hell is already in ruins because of you. Couldn’t you live happily enough here?”

“Yes, I suppose. I’ve spent a lot of time here, and there’s a lot more to come. But once it’s over, I mean…”

Guffin shrugged.

“That eternity is no different from an instant. Isn’t it? Time seems to keep flowing, but when we finally reach the end of the future…”

When the moment comes that only the past remains:

“All your memories are just one feeling held in zero seconds. How long you lived means nothing.”

That was why.

“Any existence that can’t go beyond infinity is an illusion. Me, you, your siblings…”

Guffin pointed at Lucifer.

“Ikael too.”

Originally, there had been nine archangels.

The eight concepts that make up the world, and one more concept, their opposite.

Lucifer, archangel of chaos.

“Of course, Ankera didn’t make you. You were never born. You could call yourself negative energy that belongs to time that doesn’t exist and events that never happened.”

That is what lies outside the law.

“In the great chain, you and the angels are the two halves of the law. When the angels’ concepts grow weak, chaos grows strong…”

Guffin winked.

“But that doesn’t mean you get to meet Ikael.”

Lucifer’s nose wrinkled at words that hit the mark so precisely, but when he thought about it, he was the only one who suffered for it.

“So you’re going to gamble on the wall of infinity? If you fail, it’s eternal extinction.”

“I don’t care.”

Guffin’s eyes turned serious.

“Even after six hundred gyeong hours, what remains is emptiness. If God really is indifferent, that’s fine too. It means it wouldn’t matter if I became a god myself.”

That was why he hadn’t chosen reincarnation like his companions and had stayed on as a signal.

Guffin walked toward the wall.

“Shall I get going?”

To break through infinity.

“Wait.”

At Lucifer’s word, Guffin turned his head.

“Wasn’t that enough of a goodbye?”

Lucifer got goosebumps. Guffin could still laugh even as he headed into eternity.

“Do you really think it could exist? Not a careless god, but a god who loves this world?”

“Hmm, it’s actually not that hard. That’s not really what the concept of God is. But if I were a god…”

Guffin looked up at the ceiling.

“Wouldn’t it be all right to give people hope? Even if it’s only a signal.”

Lucifer was silent for a long time.

*A messiah.*

If he could really pull it off…

“I will send you back to reality.”

At Lucifer’s unexpected words, Guffin raised an eyebrow playfully.

“Oho? After all this time? How long have you been itching to say that?”

“It isn’t a trick. Purification can’t be stopped by any means. But that is exactly why I can send you away.”

Guffin understood.

“No way. You…”

“Yes. Someone has to be purified either way. I will perish in your place. I am the only one in Hell who can stand in for data like yours.”

“Hmm.”

Guffin rested his chin on his hand and thought.

“You know what kind of pain that is, right? It will be on a whole different level from what I’ve been handling.”

“In return… there is a condition.”

Guffin laughed.

“I knew there would be.”

“If you truly want to give meaning to an empty world, share that meaning with us too.”

Because it couldn’t end as nothing but a signal.

“I understand how you feel.”

Guffin looked puzzled.

“But you’re different from humans. You have no link. You’re just a part of the system that makes up this world.”

“Ikael.”

Lucifer said it firmly.

“Give her meaning. Not just a system, but the meaning of being like you. That is my condition.”

“Ikael, huh.”

That stubborn archangel, of all people.

“I thought you hated her?”

In fact, Ikael, the beginning of the law, and Lucifer, the chaos outside it, had never met.

Just as being and nothing can never be joined.

But because of that, Lucifer hated and envied Ikael more than anyone.

He still remembered.

At the very first moment, the archangel Ikael born smiling in a brilliant explosion.

What had he felt first, back then?

*Why was I…*

*…born so ugly?*

Through the early years of Omega, Shirone knew how deep Lucifer’s resentment ran.

*Yin and yang.*

The two extremes had split apart the moment they were born.

Coming out of the memory, Lucifer went on, his voice breaking.

“I was jealous. We were born in the same place, so why was I so ugly?”

It was a matter of concept, not appearance.

“Why do I hate Ikael? Why do I think I am ugly and Ikael is beautiful?”

“So, did you figure it out?”

“Perhaps.”

Fighting Guffin for more than six hundred gyeong hours had changed Lucifer’s mind too.

“Because I can’t have her.”

Lucifer smiled sadly.

“There was only one thing I could do. But not anymore. If it’s all empty anyway…”

If emotions are only signals:

“Then I want to at least do it with love. Love is better than hate, isn’t it?”

Just as Lucifer had looked at Ikael and found her beautiful.

Shirone doubted his own senses.

*Love?*

That word, coming from the mouth of the lord of Hell.

*Ah…*

What suddenly came to mind were the last words of Igor, commander of the Demon Division.

*So this is what he meant.*

“It isn’t that there are no penitent demons at all.”

Guffin asked, “What will happen to Hell?”

“My disappearing doesn’t mean demons disappear. The initiative just passes to humans. I wonder what kind of hell the world they make will be…”

Lucifer shrugged.

“For most people like you, this wouldn’t be a bad place.”

“What if humans fall?”

“Hmph. A guy going back to reality doesn’t need to worry about Hell. A human-made ruler will rule this world anyway. Let’s call him Satan for now.”

“Satan.”

*Good luck, humans.*

“Good. After my purification, you can return to reality. With the repentance of the lord of Hell, your Ultima will grow stronger…”

Lucifer’s demonic eyes flashed.

“Ankera is strong.”

Guffin nodded.

“I know. But I won’t be alone. All the Gaians will unite and attack.”

“Ikael will not help you.”

Guffin knew how conservative and proud she was.

Lucifer’s fangs glinted.

“But you must keep your promise.”

The existence of the universe was at stake, so Guffin had no intention of breaking it lightly.

“I’ll do my best. I will never abandon Ikael, no matter what.”

Lucifer was finally satisfied.

“That is enough.”

The moment he closed his eyes and smiled at the thought of Ikael, a cold voice spoke.

“Time remaining until purification: 764 hae, 1,428 gyeong, 9,984 trillion, 129.8 billion, 11,763,329 hours.”

That was the price of repentance.

About a day after Guffin passed beyond the infinite wall and vanished from sight:

“…How much is left?”

Lucifer asked the sky.

“Time remaining until purification: 764 hae, 1,428 gyeong, 9,984 trillion, 129.8 billion, 11,763,329 hours.”

It hadn’t gone down by even an hour.

“Tch. Is that so?”

In Hell, time is converted from sensation, and the lord of Hell cannot feel pain.

“How embarrassing.”

As Lucifer looked up at the sky, a vision of Ikael appeared.

“Ahh…”

It must have been a love as vast as the distance between nothing and being.

“If only I could see you once.”

He reached out toward her, right in front of him, but his hand caught nothing.

For the first time in his life, a beautiful smile appeared on Lucifer’s weeping face.

“Ikael.”

A pain that tore his heart to pieces.

“I love you.”

That pain was converted completely into time.

“Aaaaaaah!”

It blew Lucifer’s body apart into black ash and drove him into the infinite wall in an instant.

Where only silence reigns.

“…Purification complete.”

A woman’s voice echoed softly and faded away.
