Face to Face (1)

“It cuts nicely.”

Even with blood running from her neck, Yu-jeong was only looking at Vulcan.

“I…!”

Air whistled out of the hole in her windpipe, and no words came out.

“What are you saying?”

Vulcan frowned, but rational as he was, he didn’t find the situation strange.

Havitz’s eyes went dark as an abyss.

“If he’s in Hell, then send me there!”

Yu-jeong kept pouring out whatever she felt, but no sound came from her moving lips.

And then, at some point:

*What?*

Her wild instincts caught that something was off.

*Something’s wrong.*

Nothing had changed from a moment ago, and yet an ominous feeling filled her head.

*This is definitely…*

The smell of death.

*I’m dying right now. But how?*

The enemy was still far away, and there was nothing near her that could hurt her.

“Did you notice?”

Havitz leaned his face in mockingly.

“You really are a monkey.”

“Who are you calling a monkey?”

Yu-jeong slowly turned her head and met Havitz face to face.

A golden light was shining in her eyes.

*Golden eyes.*

Hwaan Geumjeong: Fire Eyes, Golden Pupils.

The eyes of truth that could see through anything in the world, passed down through the bloodline of Son Oh-gong.

Meeting Yu-jeong’s eyes, Havitz muttered:

“Seeing ghosts, are you?”

“How dare you touch my body…”

As the signal of her Idea reconnected, her neck stopped being cut.

“What do you think you’re doing!”

She spun like a whirlwind and swung her staff, and a red afterimage struck Havitz’s temple.

*Kwaaaaaaaang!*

The shock wave spread into a violent storm, and Vulcan snapped out of it.

“Havitz!”

As the smoke rising from the ground cleared, Yu-jeong walked out, holding her neck.

“You’re really getting on my nerves.”

What made her angrier than the wound was that she hadn’t felt the staff connect.

“Ptoo!”

The view cleared completely, and she saw Havitz standing calmly behind her.

Around him, twelve black marks had formed in a circle like shadows.

Vulcan let out a sigh of relief.

*She can’t break Hell.*

Still, the fact that she could perceive Havitz at all made Son Yu-jeong threat enough.

“You use strange tricks.”

Blood seeping through her fingers, Yu-jeong looked from the army of Hell to Havitz.

*Damn that archangel.*

She had wondered why Ikael let her go to Satan so easily.

*She knew I’d come even if she tried to stop me.*

Havitz sniffed and came closer.

“Why fight it? Didn’t you say you wanted to see your friend? If you die, you can go to Hell.”

“Huh! Why would I die? I’m going to beat you up and bring Mortasinger back.”

Havitz raised his index and middle fingers.

“There are two ways.”

The army of Hell stirred.

*Havitz is negotiating…?*

For him to reveal Satan’s nature meant the Hwaan Geumjeong was just that much of a threat.

“A contract, or sin. If you make a contract with Satan, you can go to Hell in a living body.”

That was what had happened with Mortasinger.

“And sin means following my way. If you really want it, I will take you to Hell.”

Yu-jeong hated complicated talk, but she could tell Havitz’s words were suspicious.

“If you can take me there, why don’t I just beat you and make you take me? Why should I trust you?”

Vulcan was thinking the same thing.

*I’ve never had much interest in the underworld, but I can’t afford that anymore.*

It was no longer just a human war.

“What do you think Hell is?”

Yu-jeong didn’t answer.

“A place where the wicked go? A place where all the evil of this world flows in to suffer forever?”

Havitz raised an eyebrow.

“Nonsense.”

Good and evil are concepts humans made.

“That is not what God wants. Frankly, I don’t care about such things either.”

Vulcan asked, “Then what do you want?”

“A perfect world.”

Havitz’s purple incarnation rose like wildfire.

“That is why God made me.”

Yu-jeong leapt back a step, and where she landed, twelve black shadows bloomed.

“Si-ok.”

The world turned gray.

*Time has stopped.*

Twelve priests in black robes rose from the ground and began to sing a funeral dirge.

“Ahh, ahh, ahh!”

The music was loud enough to make the world weep, but only Yu-jeong could hear it.

“Ugh!”

The ground turned to swamp, and her body began to sink slowly.

“I will send you to Hell.”

Yu-jeong struck everywhere with the Yeouibong, but trapped in Si-ok, it was a force she couldn’t resist.

“What is this! This isn’t what you said!”

She didn’t think she fit either the contract or the sin Havitz had described.

“As I told you.”

Havitz spoke as he walked through the gray landscape.

“You yourself are evil.”

Because she spoiled God’s perfection.

“Aaaaaaah!”

Eyes blazing gold, Yu-jeong threw her upper body back, but she had already sunk up to her chin.

Somewhere in Omega.

*This place…*

Countless years had passed since the Gaians decided to go beyond infinity.

Most of those who had stood together that day had died at the end of their lives, and the ones who remained were now…

*This is Hell.*

Only Mackline Guffin was there.

“Ugh, it’s hot.”

In a fiery hell, Guffin sat cross-legged and endured the pain.

“When can I leave?”

A voice answered from the flame-covered ceiling.

“Time remaining until purification: 664 gyeong, 3,943 trillion, 30.2 billion, 39.24 million, and 68 hours.”

That was by the standard of the photon system.

“Right.”

Shirone watched Guffin with a bitter expression.

*Even after all of that passes, almost no time will have passed in reality.*

Lawless time: 0.666 seconds.

*In the end, a prison of time…*

It was an empty gap the world absolutely needed to keep its signals working normally.

*From the start, the law was never made for us.*

The world Shirone lived in was made real by combining a quantum signal called the mind with specific signals.

And Hell was nothing more than a dump where those quantum signals were processed.

*Because of the 0.666 seconds, signals from outside come through stably. Because of Hell, the mind doesn’t run out of control.*

The world was perfect.

*Is he smiling?*

An eerie question crossed Shirone’s mind.

*Is the one who made this world happy, watching this hell?*

*Look. I made this.*

“That’s something you can’t know.”

Guffin muttered it, and to Shirone it felt as if he were speaking to him.

But according to Omega’s record, they had simply happened to think the same thing.

“So I’m going to go and see for myself.”

It was going to be a very long, boring stretch of time.

“Kiaaa!”

Devils with heads jutting out above their noses were climbing the cliffs, stretching out their long arms.

“Eat! Eat!”

The inhabitants of the underworld inflict acts of pain on those who come to Hell.

*Beings with that kind of nature.*

The minds used up in reality, their emotions, flow into the world behind and are dissolved back into a pure state.

*To recycle information.*

That was how the system had worked from the beginning.

“Not bad.”

As Guffin stood up, sparks dripped from his golden hair.

“I’ll eat him!”

The instant the demons lunged, the light of Yahweh flashed from Guffin’s body.

“Kwaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaa!”

The demons caught in the light crumbled to powder, and even the flames went out completely.

Shirone was amazed.

*An infinite wizard.*

Even before the idea of magic existed, the Gaians knew how to rule over phenomena.

But only Guffin had reached the realm of Yahweh, through endless reincarnation.

“I’ve become special.”

Apostasy.

Any phenomenon that broke the balance of this world could be dragged into Hell.

Thinking of his companions who had already left the world, Guffin gave himself over to suffering willingly.

“I will lead the way.”

It was the first step of the Guffin who would later stand against Ankera on behalf of Gaia.

Being dragged into Hell, Yu-jeong began to struggle, baring her teeth.

“Kia! Kiaaa!”

The ferocious monkey’s screeches were buried under Si-ok’s funeral dirge, but astonishingly, her upper body came back out.

“Hoh.”

Havitz’s eyes twitched slightly.

*She’s coming back out?*

Once in Hell, there was no way back until the time of purification was over.

Yu-jeong knew this by instinct, so she fought with everything she had.

“You bastard! I’ll kill you! Do you know who I am?!”

The granddaughter of Son Oh-gong.

Her golden eyes glowed as if they would burst, and Yu-jeong’s body began to heat up red.

“I am a stone monkey who can overcome even a Buddha if I set my mind to it!”

By realizing her incarnation she had risen to the level of a living creature, but in truth her Idea was inorganic.

No physical force could destroy it, and there was no limit to the energy it could release.

“Kiaaaaaaa!”

The objet Yeoui, inherited from her ancestors, stretched out in a straight line along the border between reality and Hell.

*Move! Move!*

As her body burned white, Si-ok’s funeral dirge rang out even louder.

“Just a little more…!”

Suddenly the ground shook, and Yu-jeong opened her jaw as wide as it would go and screamed.

“Shut up!”

As the Yeouibong spun between the real world and the other side, the ground heaved like a tidal wave.

“What, what is it?”

The headquarters building shook.

In the commander’s office, where he had been reviewing strategy, Iruki looked up at the ceiling in surprise.

“It’s cracking! Get out!”

Just as Aromi shouted at Iruki, the door opened and a messenger came in.

“General! Urgent report!”

“What is it?”

Up to now, they had believed they could wipe out the demons within forty-eight hours.

Good or bad, the fact that something unexpected had happened made his heart pound.

“The Homeland Defense Ministry recorded a powerful earthquake. The epicenter is the Cliff of the Abyss. Magnitude 12.4.”

“What!”

Iruki slammed the table and stood up.

“What do you mean? The Cliff of the Abyss is a mountain-building zone… No, that’s not the point.”

Magnitude 12.4?

Iruki pictured what an earthquake of that size would do.

*It would tear the ground apart.*

At first he had thought about the geography, but now he hoped it was just nature’s whim.

*Yes, it has to be a natural disaster. It’s unthinkable that someone could exert that kind of power on purpose.*

It was a level of destruction impossible for any of the world powers Iruki had analyzed.

“General.”

Iruki met Aromi’s eyes, came to his senses, and bit his lip.

*Yes. That’s right.*

It couldn’t be a natural disaster.

*That ominous feeling that’s been stuck in my head for a while. It has come true.*

A commander asked, “General, what do we do now?”

“First, the extent of the damage at the Cliff of the Abyss…”

Iruki stopped and shook his head.

There was no need to check. It had surely collapsed to the point that the demons couldn’t move through it.

*Even so, I have no choice but to push ahead.*

Having lost the best position to detonate the elemental bomb, Iruki looked over the world map.

“The second site for the operation is…”

As he pointed with a baton, Iruki’s fingertips were trembling badly.

“The capital of Tormia. Baska.”

It was a great city, home to more than half of the kingdom’s population.
