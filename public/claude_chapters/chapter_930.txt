*I see.*

Shirone had met Argo when he fell into Apocalypse because of Valhalla Action’s debt.

That was also proof that the eternal life program had never actually run.

Of course, to reach that memory, Shirone would have to take in a great deal more of Omega.

*It is not truly infinite.*

He had already grasped how the Gaians reasoned, so he could understand it without living through it.

*Even if time is split endlessly, time in the higher dimension still flows in the end.*

Suddenly he remembered the horrible sight of entering World No. 19000 of Andre’s labyrinth.

Every human being had lost their reason and lived squirming like worms in a narrow passage.

Perhaps that was the end the eternal life program would bring humanity.

*The eternal life program cannot last forever.*

That was why Guffin wanted to break the chains of reincarnation and leap to the higher dimension.

*But Ankera does not acknowledge the world outside.*

And the era Shirone lived in was proof that Ankera would stand in the way of Gaia’s escape.

*This is the beginning of the war.*

In other words, the beginning of a tragedy.

“Hmm.”

Yohrham sighed.

“Does it have to be us?”

No one answered, but for the Gaians, answers weren’t really needed to communicate.

“If not us, then who?”

Guffin stood and looked out at the gathered crowd.

“Let’s go. To the end of the world.”

The moment he finished, every Gaian there raised a hand.

“Waaah!”

As a huge roar shook the walls, Yohrham shouted:

“The Gaians will stretch on to infinity!”

Shirone knew how much Gaia would pay for this decision.

*But they are already prepared.*

Why weren’t they afraid?

*Because they are one.*

Their ages, genders, and natures were different, but they reasoned by the same perfect standard.

It was a different kind of unity from the dragons, who could only read each other’s information.

*The pinnacle of intellect.*

What would it feel like if everyone in the world stood behind you, and you, one individual, became the whole?

*It’s amazing.*

Know what is right, and do it.

Tears ran from Shirone’s eyes. He had had to carry so much alone to save the world.

*I want to be there too.*

He wanted to bury himself in that crowd of Gaians.

*It’s too hard. It’s too lonely.*

Someday, the people of Shirone’s world would also be united as one.

After countless conflicts, and objection after objection, the day would come when they found the answer.

*I have to fight.*

Shirone wiped away his tears and reached again for Omega.

Countless people were celebrating Gaia’s new beginning, each in their own way.

“Beyond infinity.”

Through Omega’s signal, Guffin’s voice pierced Shirone’s mind.

Left alone in Antarctica, where every living thing had vanished, Uriel thought over Miro’s words.

*My destruction has no emotion?*

Since when had he, the strongest archangel in the universe, been shaken by a human’s words?

Even so, he couldn’t bring himself to leave, because what she had said was true.

*The archangel Ikael.*

She was the one cause behind both Kariel and Uriel, the archangels of birth and destruction.

*I don’t care who you love.*

What was amazing was only that an archangel could love someone at all.

“What is love?”

Kariel had been obsessed with that question, turned it over countless times, and in the end destroyed himself.

*Did he figure it out after all?*

With his only counterpart gone, would Uriel find the answer Kariel had longed for?

*Unpleasant.*

Adrias Miro.

Every time he thought of her, an indescribable, unpleasant feeling shook his asteroid.

“Next time, I will kill you.”

By killing her, not destroying her.

Opening the halo of judgment, he gathered a concept of destruction on a completely different level than before.

“Is this all right, human?”

The moment the white flash struck, Uriel’s body pierced straight through Antarctica.

*Kurrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrr!*

It was a force that could be counted on the fingers of one hand in the entire history of the planet.

“Ugh!”

Before her desperate words could even leave her mouth, Taeseong’s eyes rolled back and she collapsed.

“Taeseong!”

The Four Great Saints, gathered at the Temple of Earth, rushed to her.

“Steady!”

She was the incarnation of the planet, and yet now they feared that even that incarnation might disappear.

“This is insane.”

Beneath the Temple of Earth, on the planet below, Antarctica was disappearing.

Amanta said, “This is only the beginning. If beings like that run wild here, it’s only a matter of time before we’re destroyed.”

“Fried…”

At Taeseong’s voice, Fried, who was holding her up, quickly bent down.

“You’re awake?”

“There is no time. At this rate, the planet will stop working. Please take me to the Central Continent.”

“The Central Continent?”

Minerva raised an eyebrow.

“There’s no need for you to go to the battlefield. The stars of the Ivory Tower are enough.”

“No. There is someone I truly need to meet.”

Fried asked, “You mean Shirone?”

“No.”

Taeseong shook her head and narrowed her eyes.

“Ogent Lian. I must meet him.”

Like a meteor tearing through the atmosphere, a red-hot body streaked across the sky.

“Those bastards…”

Son Yu-jeong, second in the order of the Ten Commandments.

“You’re picking on my friend?”

Any ordinary creature would have burned up already, but her temperature kept rising without end.

“There.”

Finding Satan was easy.

Even from high in the sky, she could see the infamous two-hundred-million-strong army of Hell gathered and marching.

“An intruder!”

Demons watching the sky flew at her with blazing tridents.

“Hmph. Monsters?”

In more than ten thousand years of life, she had met demons from the other world until she was sick of them.

“Yeoui.”

Her red staff spun rapidly and began to stretch to an enormous length.

“Invincible Windmill.”

As the staff, four kilometers across, whirled, the sky turned deep red.

How far it could extend matched exactly the range its user’s scale allowed.

“Out of my way!”

In theory it was close to infinite, and the Yeouibong, losing none of its force, swept the demons away.

“What?”

Havitz and the others, who had entered the Cliff of the Abyss, all looked back toward the far side of the sky at once.

“It’s her.”

A huge red sphere, clearly visible from the ground, was coming toward them, sweeping demons aside.

Vulcan’s eyes turned serious.

*Size doesn’t matter.*

Or maybe size did matter?

At a rough estimate, she had the strength to swing a four-kilometer staff fast enough to leave afterimages.

“Here I come.”

Yu-jeong crashed down feet first onto a demon and skidded as if on a sheet of ice.

“Kuaaaaa!”

The brigade commander, his face torn apart, died in agony, and Yu-jeong turned to face the enemy.

As if a red-hot boulder had fallen, the air around her grew scorching.

“Haaah.”

The demons watched hot steam pour out of Yu-jeong’s mouth.

“Satan. Come out.”

Vulcan thought it through.

First, she had clearly come alone. He couldn’t sense any army with her.

Second, she was dangerous all the same.

“Hurry up…”

Yu-jeong took a deep breath, clenched her fists, bared her teeth, and roared.

“I said come out!”

There was something beastly in her voice, a violence that seemed to rip through the air.

As Vulcan frowned, wondering what to do about Yu-jeong, a horse stepped forward.

“Satan? That would be me.”

Havitz stroked his mustache as he spoke.

“It’s you?”

Even Yu-jeong fell silent for a moment.

Her animal sense of smell caught a chaos that reached back to primeval times.

“Well, fine.”

As she spun the Yeouibong in her fingers, Yu-jeong’s body vanished from sight.

*I’ll beat him up first, then…*

The moment she reached Havitz and swung her staff at his side:

*What?*

Trailing a blurred afterimage, a girl whose eyelids had been cut away appeared in Yu-jeong’s vision.

Natasha knocked the staff aside with the back of her hand, dropped low, and slipped inside Yu-jeong’s reach.

*She blocked it.*

Yu-jeong was startled.

Most of the demons couldn’t even follow her movements, and yet this girl was meeting her eyes directly.

*Is that so?*

As Yu-jeong thrust with the staff, ripples in the air showed clearly in the slowed flow of time.

Natasha twisted her upper body to dodge and drove a fist into Yu-jeong’s stomach.

*Why is she so hard?*

Natasha’s strength hardly seemed to belong to a living creature, yet it was her own fist that felt like it was breaking.

*Hmph, with a fist like this…*

The moment Yu-jeong tensed her stomach and pulled her staff back to counterattack:

*Now!*

With her fist still pressed to Yu-jeong’s stomach, Natasha kicked off the ground and bent her arm.

Like a hammer striking a nail, she drove her elbow in with the force of her hips, and Yu-jeong was shoved away.

*Got her!*

Having broken her enemy’s balance, Natasha flew at Mach speed and unleashed a barrage of attacks.

Havitz’s army listened to sounds they couldn’t see and pictured the fight.

“Hah!”

Natasha’s fist struck Yu-jeong squarely on the chin.

*Kwak-kwa-kwa-kwa-kwak!*

Yu-jeong was dragged across the ground a long way before she caught herself and looked ahead.

*Natasha has to go this far?*

As Vulcan judged the situation without showing any emotion, Natasha raised the backs of her hands.

“You’re really strong.”

Blood was streaming down the backs of her hands, and broken bones were sticking out.

Vulcan’s expression crumpled.

*The kind that’s hard to break.*

If Natasha’s fists couldn’t break her, she had to be considered nearly immune to physical attacks.

Yu-jeong straightened up and rested the Yeouibong on her shoulder.

“Heh. You’re pretty fast too.”

It wasn’t impossible to find a way, but Vulcan didn’t want to waste energy before the coming battle.

“What do you want?”

Yu-jeong rubbed her slightly swollen cheek and wiped the blood from her lips.

“Let my friend go. Mortasinger. You dragged him into Hell, didn’t you?”

“Mortasinger?”

Vulcan, who had no interest in the underworld, turned to Havitz.

“Never heard of him.”

There was no way Havitz didn’t know Mortasinger. Satan had taken him to Hell personally.

It had only been a way to mock Yahweh, and even remembering it was a bother.

“What?”

Yu-jeong wrinkled her nose like a monkey and bared her teeth.

“Then I’ll make you remember.”

As terrifying killing intent spread, Vulcan saw what was coming and quickly held out a hand.

“Wait. Havitz…”

But before he could finish, Satan’s purple incarnation surged out of Havitz’s body.

*Vanishing.*

Once every causal tie to Havitz was cut, no one could perceive him.

Yu-jeong didn’t notice anything wrong either.

“What are you going to do? Hurry up and bring my friend, before I destroy everyone here.”

Havitz got off his horse, drew a longsword, and walked around behind Yu-jeong.

“You’re an interesting one.”

The cold blade touched the back of her neck.

“No… you’re not human, are you?”

He slowly drew the sword from side to side, but her skin didn’t cut, as if the blade were dull.

“Aha.”

Havitz’s eyes curved like crescent moons.

“You’re connected.”

He attacked the Idea directly.

*Shff. Shff.*

And at last, red blood began to run down Yu-jeong’s slender neck.
