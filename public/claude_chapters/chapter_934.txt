Chapter 934

Face to Face (4)

~*~

A day passed.

“Owww.”

When Amy got out of bed and moved as usual, pain surged through every muscle in her body.

*It feels like I fell off a cliff.*

Holding her aching back, she limped over and looked out the window.

Nane and Shura had gotten up early and were tending a small vegetable garden.

*What are they doing?*

The world was heading toward ruin, and the Buddha of this age was plowing a field.

Sensing her gaze, Nane straightened his back and looked toward the cabin.

When she saw Nane’s smile, Amy remembered what he had said yesterday.

*He should at least say something that makes sense.*

She could understand wanting to take on Shirone’s heart in order to understand him.

*Is that what love is?*

The moment she asked herself that, it suddenly struck her.

*Why do I love Shirone?*

Maybe, as Nane said, simply giving your heart was all love really was.

“Ugh, fine! It’s none of my business, is it?”

More worrying than that was what Shura had heard yesterday: the heavenly army had arrived.

*Havitz alone is overwhelming. Iruki must be having a hard time. No, it’s worse than that…*

It would be the worst war in history.

Having thought that far, Amy looked at Nane with a different feeling than a moment ago.

His face was covered in tattoos and looked vicious, but his smile was as innocent as ever.

*I don’t think he’s a bad person.*

She didn’t even want to imagine what would have happened if Havitz had caught her instead of Nane.

*If he were on Shirone’s side…*

Above all, Nane had swallowed the dream of Ankera, who ruled the heavenly army.

As if reading Amy’s thoughts, Nane shook his head with a mischievous look.

*When are you going to say you love me?*

*No, no.*

Getting her hopes up like that was dangerous, so Amy shook her head.

*I have no choice but to fight.*

Her body still hurt too much to even run, but magic wasn’t done with the body.

When she opened the cabin door and went out, Shura stood up and pulled off the hood she was wearing.

“Done lazing around? Hurry up and help.”

“Why me?”

“Those who don’t work don’t eat. Didn’t you know? If you don’t want to starve, pick up stones.”

She wouldn’t die from skipping food for a day or so, but she didn’t want to be childishly stubborn either.

“Then give me a hoe too…”

Nane cut in.

“Are you feeling all right?”

“Don’t worry about it. I’ll pay for my meals.”

Nane smiled as Amy pouted and puffed out her cheeks.

“Guests don’t need to work. Leave this to us and do what you need to do. If you go into the woods over there, there’s a good place to train your mind.”

“I’m fine. And please don’t pretend to be nice to me. It gives me goosebumps.”

Nane wasn’t offended.

“Don’t say that. Just go. You’re a soldier, the world is on the edge of destruction, and if you stay here leisurely plowing fields, you’ll explode with frustration. That isn’t good for your body either.”

Remembering her mission, Amy came to her senses.

“You’re right. What am I doing here? Feed me or don’t, I’m going to go train.”

Without waiting for an answer, she left, and Shura threw down her hoe and lost her temper.

“Ugh, annoying! She’s nothing special.”

“The Fire Realm is not nothing.”

Shura shut her mouth.

At one time Amy might have been just a strong wizard, but now she was a system.

*That pillar of fire, back then.*

She could still see the enormous flames that had shot into the sky from the Kanian Plateau.

“Hmph!”

Shura glared at Nane.

“Then isn’t that all the more reason not to help her? Someday she’ll be our enemy.”

“Didn’t I tell you? If I can’t understand Shirone, I can’t become a true Buddha. The same goes for Shirone. You must have grasped the truth of emptiness by now.”

“Isn’t it just because she’s pretty?”

“Haha! I can’t deny that.”

Shura’s eyes went wide.

“What kind of Buddha are you?”

Nane stroked Shura’s hair.

“Pretty is pretty. The first quality needed to face the truth is not turning away from the facts.”

The Buddha’s words were always right, so she should have accepted them this time too.

*Why am I like this?*

From the moment Nane confessed to Amy, what was this feeling weighing on her heart?

*Why am I at the Buddha’s side?*

For the first time, that small fact felt bigger than the secrets of the world.

Shura never asked, and Nane went back to plowing the field with a polite smile.

*Ignition!*

The moment she set up Ignition, the foundation of fire magic, flames shot up from Amy’s body.

“Whoa.”

Power so great it surprised even her.

*Is this incarnation art?*

It felt as if she had become the flame itself, rather than a wizard who handled flame.

“But it isn’t enough.”

She couldn’t fight the heavenly army without the power of the magic she had cast on the Kanian Plateau.

“Phew. Phew.”

She had already crossed the line of death once, and she was afraid to take the first step…

“Here goes.”

Amy was a soldier.

*Coffin of Cowardice.*

As her focus dug endlessly toward cowardice, her consciousness faded as if her brain had opened up.

*I can’t give up!*

If she couldn’t control the inertia of coercion, she would die the way she nearly had on the Kanian Plateau.

“Ugh!”

Her red eyes flashed thirty times a second, but it was impossible to control, and the flames kept growing.

*No! No!*

The moment she realized the line of control had snapped, the flames burned as if doused in oil.

“Sermon. Calm.”

Nane’s voice resonated through the world, and the flames died down as if the world itself were pressing on them.

“Haah!”

It was like being held underwater until you were about to drown, and then someone grabbing you by the hair and pulling you out.

“Haa! Haa!”

Amy’s spirit snapped back into the world.

After gasping for a long time with dilated pupils, she slowly turned her head.

Nane was scratching an eyebrow with a puzzled look.

“That’s a hard one. I suggested mental training, not training with your life on the line.”

“What does it matter?”

She wanted to argue fiercely, but his sermon had saved her, so her voice came out quiet.

Nane walked over with his hands behind his back.

“Your incarnation is dangerous. It’s actually rare for a human to awaken an element of the natural world as an incarnation. Except for Shirone.”

Shirone’s Hexa was light itself.

“Of course, you’re unusual too. Perhaps a fire spirit lives in your mind.”

*Jack O’Lantern.*

The fire spirit Shirone had given her as a gift, from the edge of Heaven, had grown up alongside Amy.

“You mean it showed up as an incarnation?”

“You’ve kept a higher affinity for fire than others. Of course, it’s a level you can’t reach without hard training. It’s a double-edged sword. If you don’t handle it properly, it will hurt the one who wields it.”

*Incarnation isn’t simple.*

From what had just happened, she could tell it didn’t get stronger through repetition the way magic did.

It needed a special realization, a way of controlling the mind beyond the mind.

*That’s why I can’t meditate right now. No, will I ever be able to?*

Amy knew better than anyone that she was far from a seeker.

Nane asked:

“May I help you?”

“It’s fine. I’ll figure it out myself.”

Especially after what she had heard yesterday, she didn’t want to owe Nane anything more.

“Don’t make it complicated. Helping someone who has lost their way is only natural. It has nothing to do with Shirone.”

He was the one closest to being right in this whole world.

“I know. I still don’t want to.”

Nane gave a deflated laugh.

“You really are stubborn. Then I’ll give you a hint. You weren’t far off. Of course, there’s no such thing as big or small, but enlightenment is like that. A fleeting flash. A problem that can be solved in an instant with a slight twist.”

“Just like that?”

“Yes. It’s up to you. Just set your stubbornness down for a little while.”

Amy stuck out her lower lip.

“Then shall we try again? Don’t worry about anything. Open your incarnation all the way.”

Nane stretched out his right arm.

“Sermon Wall.”

An ocher wall spread out like a fan and closed off the surrounding scenery like a net.

Amy looked at him suspiciously, and Nane shrugged.

“I at least need to protect my garden.”

“Hmph. Fine.”

She hadn’t understood the sermon, but something stirred when he told her to open her incarnation all the way.

*I was scared.*

*This time, I’ll control it.*

*Coffin of Cowardice.*

Her red eyes lit up, and flames rose around Amy again.

“Haah!”

That was what made it so hard.

*I know it in my head…*

She had thought her heart was lighter than a feather, but fear still lingered.

Her mind was being pulled in.

“Hngh!”

Again the flames grew out of control and struck the ceiling of the wall Nane had made.

*I have to stop here…*

Then Nane’s voice rang in her head.

“Don’t stop.”

*What?*

“You are fire. It won’t stop until it has burned everything. Grow bigger. It grows without end.”

*Without end?*

A flash went through Amy’s mind.

*I see!*

The only thing that can control fire.

“Yaaaaah!”

Amy broke through the limit of her fear and, in a trance, raised her firepower even higher.

*Yes! Burn! Burn everything!*

As if she had turned into a bomb, Amy’s mind was wrapped in the will of fire.

“Huh, uh, uh, uh!”

The moment the will of fire pushed past the speed of cowardice, a huge release swept through her.

“Yes.”

Nane smiled.

“Exactly.”

When Amy came back to herself, all she could see were flames surrounding her.

“This is…”

They were small, but the flames pointed outward in every direction like a porcupine’s quills.

“The Will of Fire.”

Nane spoke as he lowered his hand.

“The true meaning of the Fire Realm is to become fire before it consumes you. Remember: what holds fire back is a bigger fire.”

“I… did…”

Amy, who had been listening in a daze, threw up her hands and shouted.

“I did it! I did it!”

She had controlled the fire.

She had finally reached the level she had longed for since her days at magic school.

“I-I did it! I…!”

Watching her run around with tears in her eyes, Nane smiled happily.

“You really love magic. Congratulations.”

Amy stopped.

“…So I got help after all.”

“You did it yourself. I was a wizard once too, so I know how much you wanted this. And saying a few words doesn’t count as helping.”

“It’s different when they’re the Buddha’s words. Honestly, if it had been anyone but you, I wouldn’t have risked my life following them.”

“If you put it that way…”

As Nane scratched his head in embarrassment, Amy felt a little bad.

*Right. A favor should be accepted as a favor.*

“I truly have no ulterior motive. You’re the one who risked your life, and what kind of seeker would turn away from a being who has lost their way…”

“Thanks.”

Amy smiled.

“This time, I’ll admit you helped.”

Nane blinked at the unexpected answer for a moment, and then he understood.

*I see.*

He felt as if he understood Shirone a little.
