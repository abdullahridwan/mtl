Chapter 932

Face to Face (2)

~*~

Shura’s eyes widened.

*I’d rather you just killed me.*

Nane had chanted scripture all night, and now the mountains, rivers, and trees were trembling with it.

Shura was out at the edge of the hideout made with Gestalt’s ability, yet the sound didn’t seem to fade at all.

*Kurrrrrr.*

A vibration coming from far away brought Shura back to herself for the first time in a long while.

“Huh?”

Seismic waves rushed in at high speed and tore through the ground where Shura stood.

The Gestalt collapsed, the veil of illusion faded, and the mountain ranges stretching all the way to Baska came into view.

“What’s happening?”

She had learned a great deal about the world following in the Buddha’s footsteps.

*This isn’t natural.*

If an earthquake this big had started near the coast, a tsunami should have struck the continent.

*It’s the power of an apostate.*

Several faces flashed through Shura’s head.

*Shirone, Lian, Gaold…*

But none of them could cause an earthquake big enough to shake the continent.

“Heaven has opened.”

Naturally, the people of Heaven, which was almost a home to her, came to mind first.

*I have to tell him.*

Nane was still busy chanting, but Shura kicked off the ground and ran to him.

The cabin where Amy lay unconscious was filled with Nane’s voice.

“Mahaban-ya…”

The sounds coming from Nane’s mouth seemed to pile up like matter with weight.

“Gwanjajae…”

The moment Nane’s voice cut off, a small tremor shook the building.

“Mm.”

He hadn’t stopped just because he sensed the earthquake.

Amy had taken in so many realizations and still couldn’t wake, but now her eyes slowly opened, as if at a signal.

“…You’re awake?”

Dazed, she didn’t even register that Nane’s voice was human speech.

*Where am I?*

As if she had become an insect, she could only move her eyes left and right. She couldn’t feel her arms or legs.

*I’m sure I…*

Terrible memories came back one after another.

While escaping with her comrades, she had run into the division commander, Parkma, and they had been wiped out.

*Berik.*

He had been too talented to die like that.

*Only I survived.*

Amy bit her lip. Her life had been saved because of a man’s sacrifice.

*Some soldier I am.*

Suddenly, Nane’s face came into view.

“Are you all right?”

Amy stared at him for a moment.

*Who is this?*

His face was so covered in tattoos that it was hard to make out who he was.

But the man’s eyes looked like someone she knew and missed.

*Shirone.*

The moment she thought it, everything came flooding in, and she understood.

“Na… Nane!”

Looking down at the startled Amy, Nane smiled kindly.

“That’s right. It’s me.”

Amy hurriedly sat up.

“Aaaaaah!”

A moment later, pain like her muscles were on fire swept over her, and she collapsed back onto the bed.

“You are like a newborn child. It will hurt, and forcing yourself to move could injure your body.”

“Why are you here?”

The Buddha of this age.

She could never look kindly on someone who judged the world to be empty and tried to close off the universe.

Above all, he was clearly an enemy, the opposite of Shirone.

“You’re still unwell.”

Nane pulled the blanket over Amy, stood, and walked to the door.

“I’ll make something to eat. Until then…”

Nane stopped and slowly turned around.

“Haa. Haa.”

Amy had gotten down from the bed and was glaring at him, holding up her body as it shook with pain.

She gritted her teeth.

“Answer me. Why are you here? What did you do to me?”

Nane smiled.

“Well, this and that…”

But when Amy’s expression turned suddenly cold, he quickly corrected himself.

“You were being consumed by fire. It was so powerful it was even burning away your consciousness. So I woke you.”

“You woke me up?”

Amy remembered the situation.

Out of her mind with grief over Berik’s death, she had raised her firepower at the cost of her own life.

“How did you wake me?”

It would take far too long to explain, so Nane turned away.

“Get some rest. I’ll bring you something to eat.”

Either way, it seemed true that Nane had helped her, so Amy kept quiet for a moment.

Then her eyes flew open and she rushed at him.

*Get rid of the Buddha.*

When she thought about it, hadn’t all of this happened because Nane existed?

There was no denying he was a big reason she had been separated from Shirone.

Her crimson eyes glowed red, and a huge aura of flame rose from her hand.

As if all the dirt had been washed from her mind, her power was far greater than before.

*Even if it’s cowardly, I have no choice!*

Aiming only to subdue Nane, Amy reached out without hesitation.

“Die…!”

With a loud sound, a sword was thrust straight into Amy’s stomach.

“Sermon. Vessel.”

As the dark red sword scattered like an illusion, Amy’s eyes filled with sparks.

“Aaaaaah!”

Pain beyond anything she could imagine swept over her, and her knees hit the floor.

Hearing the scream, Shura threw the door open from outside and shouted.

“Nane!”

As Shura stood speechless at the scene in front of her, Nane turned his head toward Amy.

“Yahweh makes what does not exist. The Buddha realizes what does exist. If it hurts…”

“Hngh!”

Amy raised her head, arms wrapped around herself, her eyes still unbowed.

“You may think of it as my pain.”

Nane had experienced everything in this world, so his voice worked as truth.

Shura stepped in.

“When did she wake up?”

“She came to just a little while ago. More importantly, it seems there was a big earthquake.”

“It must be the heavenly army. Perhaps they came here to find me.”

Nane smiled meaningfully.

He might have swallowed Ankera’s dream, but his philosophy was different from what the angels pursued.

“They’ll find us eventually. Even Gestalt’s ability can’t hide us from the angels’ senses.”

Even in her pain, Amy overheard their conversation.

*The heavenly army has come?*

If they had joined the fight while she was out of action, there was no hope for humanity.

“Let’s wait for now.”

Before Nane could open the door and leave, Amy asked:

“Wait. What are you going to do with me?”

Someone who had hidden behind a veil all this time would never suddenly save her life for no reason.

*I know why.*

Because she was Shirone’s lover.

“Even if you hold on to me, you won’t get what you want. I’d rather die than be used.”

She had no intention of becoming a hostage.

“Haa. You really are stubborn.”

Then again, that was exactly why she had caught Nane’s interest among countless living beings.

“As for what I plan to do with you…”

Nane looked up at the ceiling, lost in thought, then met Amy’s eyes and smiled.

“First, we need to feed you.”

The cabin had no table, so the three of them sat in a circle on the floor to eat.

It was a simple meal of greens, but to Amy every bit of it was nourishment she needed.

“Ugh.”

The pain she hadn’t felt while facing Nane kept tormenting her.

“It would be best not to push yourself for a while. Once you’re back to normal, do as you like.”

Amy set her dishes down.

“I don’t understand. You know who I am and you’re still doing this? What am I to you?”

Shura pointed at her.

“How dare you talk to the Buddha like that! Did you forget we saved your life?”

“Then tell me why you saved it! If you just saved me, then what about everyone the demons killed until now?”

“That’s…”

To be honest, Shura had her own doubts.

Nane began.

“The reason I saved you…”

“Go on. It’s because I’m Shirone’s lover.”

“What?”

When Shura first heard it, she was stunned.

“Shirone and I could be called partners in seeking. Our directions are different, but the distance we have to travel is the same. I respect him personally. I was curious. What kind of woman is so irreplaceable to the Yahweh of universal love?”

“You’ve misunderstood. Shirone is a friend I met in school. It has nothing to do with universal love.”

“Is that so.”

With that short answer, Nane picked up his bowl again and brought a few grains of rice to his mouth.

Amy, meanwhile, kept glaring at him without touching her food.

In the end, Nane gave up on eating and went on.

“If I threatened to kill you, would Shirone give in to me?”

The answer came instantly.

“No. Never.”

“Wrong.”

Nane shook his head.

“Shirone would give in. He could give up the whole world to save you.”

Amy’s eyes widened.

“So that’s why you saved me? Because if I die, you can’t blackmail Shirone?”

“No. The opposite. I want to understand Shirone. I want to take in everything about him.”

What happened after that didn’t matter.

“So…”

If his philosophy still held even after he understood Shirone’s universal love, then he would be a true Buddha.

“May I love you?”

A grain of rice flew out of Shura’s mouth.

“Pfft!”

Amy was flustered too, and a long, awkward silence filled the cabin.

“No.”

Amy came to her senses.

“Does that even make sense? Why would you love me? What do you know about me?”

“You must know by now. And isn’t loving someone a personal freedom?”

Amy thought of Berik.

“Of course it is, but it isn’t me you love. You love the person Shirone loves.”

“Yes. But everyone is the same. There is nothing in this world anyone loves without a reason. I am sure of it. At first it is something small. Then the feeling grows and grows until it can’t be traded for anything. You forget that it was the reason, and it simply becomes precious.”

“I am not forcing anything. Recover in peace. I only answered honestly because you asked why I saved you.”

Out of consideration for Amy, Nane left first.

*What is going on?*

Amy was chewing her rice in a daze when she felt a stinging glare on her forehead.

She looked up. Shura was staring at her, clearly displeased.

“Must be nice to have the whole world. Yahweh and the Buddha both. At this rate you’ll unify the universe.”

“What? What did I do?”

Shura knew it wasn’t Amy’s fault, but she was strangely upset.

“Anyway, hurry up and recover. Once the heavenly army starts moving, the world will end. Even if you’re going to die, shouldn’t you see your lover first?”

Amy couldn’t bring herself to nod, but she knew Shura was right that recovering was what mattered.

Annoyed that Amy only glared and said nothing, Shura threw down her dishes and walked to the door.

“You wash the dishes.”

The door slammed shut.
